Ch. 301–400 Summaries

Lord of the Mysteries

Backlund Beginnings
301Klein investigates a Numinous Episcopate resurrection ritual in a forest, saves teenagers from a zombie, and interrogates Kapusky Reid about his involvement.
302Klein attends a Beyonder gathering, sells the Spring of Elves marrow crystal to the Apothecary, and receives a clue to obtain the Apothecary formula from Bishop Utravsky.
303Klein interrogates Kapusky Reid at his home, learning about an old gentleman who taught him the Spirit Dance, and finds white feathers in the gentleman's grave.
304Klein discovers white feathers in the grave, divines their origins, and warns Kapusky to stop dabbling in mysticism, taking the copper whistle.
305Isengard Stanton hires Klein to assist in investigating the serial murder case, offering a bounty of 2,000 pounds.
306Klein analyzes old unsolved serial murders for clues, suggesting the killer might have prior experience, and shares his findings with the detective team.
307Klein meets Bishop Utravsky at the Harvest Church, who requests help to kill his past self using the Mental Terror Candle in exchange for the Apothecary formula.
308Klein battles the past Utravsky, a Sequence 6 Dawn Paladin, in a dream using the Mental Terror Candle and defeats him through strategic use of his Magician abilities.
309Klein succeeds in defeating past Utravsky, receives the Apothecary formula and the Master Key as rewards, and returns home.
310Klein learns about the National Atmospheric Pollution Council from his landlords and receives a letter from Isengard Stanton with updates on the serial murder investigation.
311The Tarot Club gathers, with new member Fors Wall (The Magician) joining, and Klein, as The World, requests information on the Devil and Guardian Sequences.
312Fors purchases the Apothecary formula from The World and learns the acting method from Justice, agreeing to pay 200 pounds for the knowledge.
313The Sun asks about the seven gods, and members exchange information; Klein learns about the King of Demonic Wolves Flegrea through The World.
314Klein divines the origins of the Master Key, seeing a vision of an Abraham family member losing control, and plans to investigate further.
315Klein returns to the Harvest Church, meets the imprisoned vampire Emlyn White, and obtains information about the Master Key's origins from him.
316Emlyn White reveals that the Master Key was stolen from 32 Verdi Street, and Klein divines the truth, planning a cautious investigation.
317Klein visits the Roselle Memorial Exhibition at the Royal Museum, observes a bookmark in Roselle's manuscript, and suspects it might be a Card of Blasphemy.
318Klein prays to The Fool for help in verifying the bookmark, and the request is passed to Audrey Hall to assist during her visit to the exhibition.
319Audrey Hall visits the Roselle Memorial Exhibition, uses her earring to damage the bookmark subtly, and confirms it has an abnormal reaction, reporting to The Fool.
320Klein prepares to steal the bookmark from the Royal Museum, using the Master Key and his spirit form to infiltrate the building under cover of night.
321Klein successfully steals the bookmarks from the Royal Museum, including a Card of Blasphemy, using his spirit form and escapes to the gray fog. While returning home, he encounters a murder scene with a Devil dog.
322Klein narrowly escapes the Devil dog by shouting for help and using a Paper Figurine Substitute. He meets a mysterious woman who interrogates him but lets him go. He returns home and confirms the bookmark is a Card of Blasphemy.
323Klein unlocks the Card of Blasphemy using 'Bernadette' in ancient Feysac, revealing it to be the Dark Emperor card with sequences from Lawyer to Dark Emperor, including potion formulas and advancement rituals.
324Audrey learns about the Card of Blasphemy from Mr. Fool and thanks him. Klein leaves the card on the gray fog and receives a letter from Isengard Stanton about the serial murder.
325Klein talks to Talim about a friend's love problem and advises Aaron Ceres to see a bishop for his bad luck, suspecting a curse from a child named Will Auceptin.
326Klein attends a circus and suspects a chubby man might be the Apothecary from a Beyonder gathering, based on his ability to read animal thoughts.
327While on a carriage, Klein is pulled into a special battle environment where the Devil dog is killed by official Beyonders using a Sealed Artifact. He escapes detection by using a double.
328Klein investigates the aftermath of the Devil dog's death, has dinner with Jurgen Cooper, and receives another letter from Isengard Stanton confirming the murderer's death and bounty distribution.
329Klein goes to Bravehearts Bar to find Kaspars, but finds the gang leader dead and a gruesome scene with claw marks. He meets Sharron and Maric, who ask for his help in killing a Wraith named Steve.
330Sharron and Maric explain their background as defectors from the Rose School of Thought and propose a mission to kill Steve in exchange for mysticism knowledge, detailing their philosophy of suppressing desires.
331Maric details the Prisoner pathway sequences (Prisoner, Lunatic, Werewolf, Zombie, Wraith) and their curses. The target Steve is a Wraith with the Sealed Artifact 'Scarlet Lunar Corona' that restrains Sharron.
332Klein asks for time to think and uses a Notary Certificate to promise secrecy about Sharron and Maric's information. He agrees to help after divination above the gray fog.
333Audrey attends a Beyonder gathering with Fors and Xio, looking for the Spectator formula but doesn't find it. They suspect being tracked, possibly by the Psychology Alchemists.
334Klein attends a Beyonder gathering, sells the Barbarian formula for purifying, demon hunting, and exorcism bullets, and hears a request for an adult Black Widow Spider Silk Gland, a Demoness of Pleasure ingredient.
335Klein explores 32 Verdi Street, finds a wraith, uses Azik's copper whistle and purifying bullets to defeat it, and discovers a notebook about the Abraham family and the Apprentice, Trickmaster, Astrologer formulas.
336Klein reads the notebook, learning about the Abraham family's curse tied to Mr. Door's pleas during the full moon, and the formulas for Apprentice, Trickmaster, and Astrologer sequences.
337Fors receives the Trickmaster formula from Mr. World via Mr. Fool. Klein gets his bounty from the serial murder case and learns about Emlyn White's disappearance from detective Stuart.
338Audrey is approached by Lady Norma about mysticism experts, possibly from the Psychology Alchemists. Klein tests the Master Key on the gray fog during the full moon and hears Mr. Door's pleas.
339Sharron takes a sedative from Klein for Maric to test. Klein asks for battle locations for the mission. Audrey meets psychologists Alucard and Escalante, suspected Psychology Alchemists members.
340Klein practices at the Quelaag Club, talks to Aaron Ceres and Stelyn Sammer. Fors prays to Mr. Fool during the full moon to avoid ravings and shares her backstory of becoming a Beyonder.
341In the Tarot Club, Audrey privately reports to The Fool about contact with the Psychology Alchemists and learns the Dark Emperor card is a Card of Blasphemy. Fors obtains the Trickmaster formula from The World.
342Alger advises Derrick to avoid a dangerous mission to the Fallen Creator's temple by pretending to lose control, revealing the risks of Shepherd Elder Lovia.
343Fors attends a Beyonder gathering but finds the Band of Light ring too expensive. Derrick drains his spirituality to feign losing control, and Audrey is introduced to psychiatrist Escalante Oseleka.
344Klein purchases the Sun Brooch at a gathering for 2,000 pounds and agrees to help Sharron and Maric assassinate Steve.
345Klein tests the Sun Brooch and meets reporter Mike Joseph, who hires him for protection during an investigation in East Borough.
346Klein prepares for the ambush at West Balam dock with Sharron and Maric, setting up explosives and using a Paper Figurine Substitute.
347Maric lures Steve and his allies to the dock. Sharron attacks, but the Scarlet Lunar Corona weakens her. Klein intervenes, revealing Steve's location with the All-Black Eye.
348Klein battles Steve, using the Sun Brooch to resist possession. Sharron uses her Door of Mysteries item, and Klein sets off explosives to disrupt the fight.
349Steve uses the Biological Poison Bottle, but Klein redirects the Scarlet Lunar Corona to Sharron, who regains strength and helps kill Jason.
350Klein and Sharron kill Steve with combined attacks, and Tyre is eliminated. Klein escapes to Lever Cathedral after setting off remaining fireworks.
351The Machinery Hivemind investigates the battle scene. Klein returns home, studies his spoils, and reads the Book of Secrets, which worships the moon, not the Evernight Goddess.
352Klein and Mike begin their investigation in East Borough, interviewing Old Kohler and witnessing the death of an elderly woman from poverty.
353They explore East Borough further, seeing overcrowded apartments and the struggles of laundry maids, highlighting the harsh living conditions.
354Klein, Mike, and Old Kohler visit the factory district, observing lead poisoning risks and protests against new textile machines.
355Derrick is isolated in the City of Silver's spire and learns from a former exploration team captain about encountering a man named Amon forty-two years ago.
356Klein hires Old Kohler as an informant for East Borough. Audrey finds a survey report on living conditions in her father's study.
357Klein attends a matchmaking banquet hosted by Stelyn Sammer, where he meets Jurgen Cooper and several ladies. Stelyn boasts about her social connections.
358Stelyn attends a luncheon at Count Hall's villa with Mary, meeting Audrey and learning the cost of a hunting hound is 450 pounds.
359In the Tarot Club, Derrick asks about Amon, and Alger explains the Amon family's history. Audrey privately exchanges Roselle diary pages with The Fool, learning about Sequence 0.
360Klein reads Roselle's early diary entries about his transmigration. The Tarot Club discusses money-making methods, and Derrick is startled by the former captain's voice in his cell.
361Derrick is attacked by a mutated former captain in his room, but Chief Colin Iliad intervenes and kills the monster. Derrick suspects the mutation is linked to the Tarot Gathering.
362Klein discovers Amon parasitizing Derrick's Spirit Body and creates a modified secret deed ritual to deal with him.
363Klein uses the gray fog's power and the Dark Emperor card to eliminate Amon from Derrick. Amon's main body notes the event.
364Colin monitors Derrick for abnormalities. Leonard learns about Parasites from a class and confronts Pallez Zoroast.
365Pallez explains his role as a Parasite. Klein investigates Emlyn White's family and finds they are law-abiding vampires.
366Klein confirms the White family's innocence. Alger digests his Seafarer potion and plans his future advancements.
367Klein handles a threatening letter for Ma'am Mary, divining it as a setup. He also divines the origin of the Biological Poison Bottle.
368Klein visits the Harvest Church and finds Emlyn White has converted to Mother Earth. Emlyn recognizes Klein by smell.
369Emlyn reveals he was coerced into conversion by the Mental Terror Candle. Klein helps him return home and secures a promise for medicine.
370Klein's efforts with Emlyn conclude, and he receives praise from Stuart, feeling some progress in digesting his Magician potion.
371Klein meets Old Kohler, hears about rumors in East Borough, and learns about Daisy's disappearance.
372Fors meets Lawrence Nord, who is searching for his brother's family, and she seeks protection from divination via Mr. Fool.
373Klein investigates Daisy's disappearance and uses divination to trace her to Capim's villa on Iris Street.
374Lawrence reflects on the Abraham family's hardships. Klein plans to rescue Daisy from Capim.
375Klein uses an artificial sleepwalking ritual to protect Fors from divination. He prepares for the rescue mission.
376Klein possesses one of Capim's henchmen to gather information on the villa's defenses and Beyonders.
377Klein divines Capim's dinner time and prepares to infiltrate the villa using the Dark Emperor card.
378Klein infiltrates Capim's villa during dinner, uses the Biological Poison Bottle to poison the occupants, and engages Harras and Katy in battle.
379The battle continues; Klein triggers a gas explosion by igniting the air, severely damaging the enemies.
380Klein rescues Daisy and other girls, leaves tarot cards on Capim's body, and escapes before High-Sequence Beyonders arrive.
381Klein divines the Beyonder characteristics from Capim's henchmen, realizes his digestion of the Magician potion is progressing, and attends a Beyonder gathering where he feels strange sensations.
382Klein suspects a High-Sequence Beyonder is present at the gathering, while Xio is contacted for a mission to investigate who has been keeping tabs on Capim.
383Audrey learns about Capim's death and the Hero Bandit Dark Emperor from her father, and Klein investigates Daisy's safe return home.
384Klein and Mike Joseph plan to interview rescued girls from Capim's trafficking, and Dr. Aaron hires Klein for protection regarding Will Auceptin.
385Derrick Berg checks on the exploration team in the City of Silver and notices abnormalities in his acquaintance Darc Regence.
386Dr. Aaron has a nightmare about Will Auceptin and shows Klein a paper crane given by Will, which seems linked to the dreams.
387Klein interprets Dr. Aaron's nightmare and accompanies Mike Joseph to interview rescued girls in East Borough, witnessing their harsh conditions.
388Klein enters Dr. Aaron's dream using a Dream Charm to explore the nightmare, seeing images of Will Auceptin threatened by a giant silver snake.
389A Nighthawk visits Klein in his dream, revealing official interest in Will Auceptin, and Klein switches the paper crane with a fake.
390Dr. Aaron and Mike Joseph visit Klein, discussing the paper crane nightmares, and the Tarot Club gathering begins.
391In the Tarot Club, Alger Wilson exchanges Roselle diary pages for information on the Dark Emperor card, revealing it to be a Card of Blasphemy.
392Derrick shares about Amon's avatar and the corrupted exploration team, and the members discuss helping him.
393Mr. Fool's angel is mentioned in purifying Amon's avatar, and the club plans to use the All-Black Eye to assist Derrick.
394Fors Wall learns about the Abraham family from The World, and they arrange to use the All-Black Eye for Derrick's mission.
395Audrey shares news of the Hero Bandit Dark Emperor, and Fors arranges to buy the Sheriff Beyonder characteristic for Xio.
396Derrick receives the All-Black Eye from Mr. Fool and plans to expose the corrupted exploration team member Darc.
397Klein reads Sights in the Spirit World, learning about the Pure Light Brotherhood and an apocalyptic prophecy from Yellow Light Venithan.
398Derrick uses the All-Black Eye to expose Darc's corruption, leading to a confrontation where Darc transforms into a monster.
399Derrick is interrogated by Aiflor and Chief Colin Iliad, successfully clearing suspicion by blaming Amon and the Fallen Creator.
400Derrick is released after interrogation, but Elder Lovia shows suspicious behavior, hinting at further mysteries in the City of Silver.
