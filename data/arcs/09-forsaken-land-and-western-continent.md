# Forsaken Land and Western Continent

- Chapters: 1201–1266
- Chapter-summary source: [https://www.novelwiki.net/lord-of-the-mysteries/summaries/chapters-1201-1300/#forsaken-land-and-western-continent](https://www.novelwiki.net/lord-of-the-mysteries/summaries/chapters-1201-1300/#forsaken-land-and-western-continent)
- Story-arc index: [https://www.novelwiki.net/lord-of-the-mysteries/story-arcs/](https://www.novelwiki.net/lord-of-the-mysteries/story-arcs/)

Klein Moretti continues his journey through the Forsaken Land of the Gods, hunting the Dark Demonic Wolf and investigating the grayish-white fog barrier believed to seal the Western Continent. He encounters Moon City, spreads the faith of Mr. Fool, and uncovers secrets related to transmigration and ancient deities.
