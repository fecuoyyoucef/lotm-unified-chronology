Ch. 701–800 Summaries

Lord of the Mysteries

Sea Voyage
701The battle against frost dragon Ulyssan continues; Groselle is severely injured but fights bravely; Klein gains control over Ulyssan's Spirit Body Threads; the team weakens the dragon with coordinated attacks.
702Ulyssan is killed; Groselle dies from his injuries; the team exits the book world; Mobet, Siatas, Snowman, and Frunziar age and die due to their long time in the book; they distribute Beyonder characteristics.
703The team decides on burial plans for the deceased; Klein takes Siatas's Beyonder characteristic; Edwina gives Klein Groselle's Travels as gratitude; they leave the Golden Dream.
704Klein investigates Groselle's Travels above the gray fog; divination reveals its origins linked to Dragon of Imagination Ankewelt; he plans to explore it later.
705Klein discusses history with Edwina; learns about mythical creatures from Azik's letter; buys the giants' key from Edwina for 5,000 pounds.
706Emlyn White visits Ian Wright to commission a search for Primordial Moon believers; encounters Sharron briefly.
707Klein and Anderson leave the Golden Dream in Bayam; Danitz decides to stay and improve himself.
708Klein buys Death Knell revolver from Ukfa Connerchris for 9,000 pounds; Anderson departs.
709Klein visits Seaweed Bar but pirates flee; meets Elland who warns him about being too eye-catching; learns about Crazy Captain Connors Viktor.
710Klein plans to find Crazy Captain using Sea God Scepter; uses divination and sea creatures to search for his ship.
711Klein attacks Crazy Captain's ship with Sea God Scepter; a demigod intervenes; Sea King Jahn Kottman arrives; Crazy Captain is killed.
712News reports Crazy Captain's death as infighting; Alger is tasked with investigating Gehrman Sparrow and Crazy Captain.
713Klein contacts Arrodes to ask about Bizarro Sorcerer potion formula; gets answers pointing to Zaratul, Antigonus notebook, and other locations.
714Tarot Club meeting; Cattleya provides Roselle's diary pages; Klein reads them, learning about Roselle's later years and pathway switching.
715Continuation of diary entries; Roselle's madness and desire for Sequence 0; Tarot Club discussions on mythical creatures.
716Alger trades primitive island coordinates for Ocean Songster potion formula; other trades in Tarot Club.
717Tarot Club free exchange; discussion on Crazy Captain's death and connections to Great Smog of Backlund.
718Klein explores Groselle's Travels in Spirit Body form; meets Groselle, Mobet, Siatas in the book world.
719Klein explores Groselle's dream in the book world, seeing Giant King's Court and its features.
720Klein leaves the dream; observes Frunziar; Alger receives Ocean Songster formula and plans advancement.
721Frank Lee writes to Klein about his new mushroom breed and summons Reinette Tinekerr to send the letter; Cattleya senses an 'Ancient Bane.' Klein replies, directing Frank's research towards food for the City of Silver.
722Klein goes to the Seaweed Bar for fake ID and tickets, encounters Silver Coin Viper Oder and a hooded man (Turani von Helmosuin), and learns that MI9 and police are searching for them.
723Klein intercepts a telegram from Admiral of Blood's crew, divines Helmosuin's location at 32 Black Pepper Avenue, and uses the Sea God Scepter to broadcast it city-wide.
724Helmosuin (Orange Light Hilarion) visits Klein, warns him about the Mother Tree of Desire, and explains his unique trait from the gray fog. Klein contacts Azik and makes a protection deal with Reinette Tinekerr.
725Klein is attacked in the forest by the Rose School of Thought demigod Shanks and Admiral of Blood Senor; Reinette Tinekerr appears and battles Shanks.
726Klein continues fighting Senor while Reinette battles Shanks, who transforms into a monstrous baby; the angel Suah attacks, and Sea King Jahn Kottman intervenes; Klein escapes by creating chaos with Tinder and a copper whistle.
727Klein and Senor engage in a prolonged battle, with Senor using a luck-enhancing item; Klein gradually gains control over Senor's Spirit Body Threads.
728Klein uses a triple combo of abilities to control and kill Senor with a Sword of Lava and Death Knell; Suah's arm appears, and Reinette fights it; Klein escapes on an undersea creature.
729The battle between demigods and angels continues; Klein escapes with Reinette's help, meets Azik who removes feather contamination, and receives advice to go to large cities for safety.
730Klein enters Groselle's Travels, finds Senor inside, turns him into a marionette, and names his luck necklace 'Scales of Luck.'
731Klein leaves Bayam for Conant City, visits Neelu Raymond to convey her father Davy Raymond's last words, and fulfills his promise.
732Klein returns to Backlund, buries Frunziar Edward's ashes in a cemetery, and reflects on his journey's end.
Backlund Resurgence
733Klein returns to Backlund as the tycoon Dwayne Dantès, visits Isengard Stanton to sell his bike company shares, and goes to the Bravehearts Bar to reestablish connections.
734Klein meets Ian at the Bravehearts Bar and learns about Gehrman Sparrow's 50,000-pound bounty; he meets Sharron, tells her about killing Senor, and offers to sell Senor's Beyonder characteristic and Scales of Luck.
735Klein and Sharron explore the Williams Street ruins, find evidence of three high-back chairs, and speculate about a ritual for Blood Emperor Alista Tudor; Klein gives Sharron a way to contact him.
736Klein and Sharron conclude that the evil spirit might have escaped and discuss the possible involvement of angels; they part ways with plans for further investigation.
737In Bayam, Danitz learns about Gehrman Sparrow's bounty and Senor's death; Elland and Oz Kent are released after interrogation by MI9.
738Klein, as Dwayne Dantès, goes to the City Family Servant Assistance Association to hire a butler, meeting Baylin and selecting three candidates for interview.
739Klein visits Saint Samuel Cathedral and donates money, then dines at the Intis Srenzo Restaurant where he sees Bernadette Gustav; Aaron Ceres and his wife turn back from the restaurant due to her discomfort.
740Fors attends a Beyonder gathering, sees a Moon Puppet sold to Windsor Behring, and uses Leymano's Travels to identify her as a Primordial Moon believer; Klein contacts Arrodes to ask about the evil spirit and butler selection.
741Klein, as Dwayne Dantès, interviews butler candidates and selects Walter, while grappling with the high costs of maintaining a tycoon's lifestyle in Backlund.
742Klein decides to rent 160 Böklund Street after evaluating options with Walter, and Emlyn White plans to monitor Rus Báthory to intercept the Moon Puppet.
743In the Tarot Club, Cattleya provides a Roselle diary page detailing the Black Emperor's resurrection, and Klein learns about Mr. Door from the entries.
744Klein sells Scales of Luck to Cattleya and offers other items like Biological Poison Bottle, while the Tarot Club members react to Admiral of Blood Senor's death.
745Transactions continue in the Tarot Club, with Klein selling the Book of Secrets to Audrey and an Interrogator Beyonder characteristic, and Alger seeks Obninsk sea monsters for advancement.
746The Tarot Club free exchange includes news about Bansy Harbor's destruction, and Emlyn confronts Windsor and the Moon Puppet, requiring Mr. Fool's intervention to survive.
747Alger arrives at Pasu Island and has a dream about an elf holding a golden cup, possibly Queen of Calamity, while Emlyn deals with the Moon Puppet's aftermath.
748Klein moves into 160 Böklund Street, hires servants including Housekeeper Taneja, and begins etiquette lessons, while Alger reports to the Church of Storms.
749Klein studies the Moon Puppet above the gray fog, learning its origins from a Primordial Moon ritual, and continues with social preparations.
750Klein visits Saint Samuel Cathedral, meets Bishop Elektra, and has an etiquette lesson with Wahana Heisen, focusing on dance and conversation.
751Leonard Mitchell investigates Sherlock Moriarty's contacts in dreams, and Fors learns about a new Beyonder gathering in East Borough from Xio.
752Klein warns Leonard Mitchell via a pigeon message to stay away, preventing further investigation into Dwayne Dantès's identity.
753Klein receives visits from Maury Macht, Riana, and Bishop Elektra, leading to an invitation to the Moon Mass and discussions on social integration.
754Klein attends the Moon Mass at Saint Samuel Cathedral, donates 300 pounds, and arranges future Bible studies with Bishop Elektra.
755Klein helps Wahana Heisen by offering a 200-pound bounty for the cheats who scammed her husband, and Walter handles the Backlund Bike Company shares negotiation.
756Xio captures the cheats and claims the bounty, while Wahana and others appreciate Klein's indirect assistance in recovering the lost cloth.
757An intruder targets Richardson in Klein's mansion, revealing a personal connection, and Leonard Mitchell has a terrifying dream encounter in Backlund's fog.
758Klein instructs Walter to investigate Wahana's trouble and offer a bounty, leading to Xio's successful mission and the recovery of most losses.
759Klein attends his first ball at Maury Macht's house, dances with Hazel Macht, and interacts with other guests including Mary Schott.
760Klein converses with Mary Schott and Hibbert Hall at the ball, learning about the Backlund Bike Company shares, and notices Hazel's strange behavior as she leaves abruptly.
761Klein attends a ball at Maury Macht's house, dances with Hazel Macht, and later deals with an infiltrator from the Black Skeleton Gang targeting his valet Richardson, with Hazel anonymously helping by knocking out the infiltrator.
762Klein investigates the infiltrator Godotpos, learning about the Black Skeleton Gang's ties to the East Balam Nation Reestablishment Society, and eliminates the gang by causing an explosion at their headquarters.
763Klein receives payment from Cattleya for the Scales of Luck via Reinette Tinekerr, while Xio attends a Beyonder gathering hosted by Mr. X, leading Fors to plan an assassination with Mr. World.
764Klein is investigated by Nighthawks through a dream interrogation by Albert, where he fabricates Dwayne Dantès's past as an adventurer in the Southern Continent.
765Derrick Berg advances to Notary in the City of Silver and selects the mystical item Thunder God's Roar, while the Tarot Club discusses various matters including Roselle's diary.
766Klein reads Roselle's diary entries about deities needing anchors to maintain rationality, Kings of Angels accommodating Uniquenesses, and a nameless island with Beyonder creatures performing strange rituals.
767In the Tarot Club, Cattleya asks if Roselle was in a secret organization, and Fors agrees to pass a message about Mr. Door to the Abraham family in exchange for payment.
768Klein notices a warning in a news article from Isengard Stanton about the shares sale, attends social events, and Derrick chooses his mystical item in the City of Silver.
769Klein, disguised as a blue cat, meets Isengard Stanton to collect money from the shares sale and uses a ritual to sacrifice the cash above the gray fog for safekeeping.
770Klein communicates with Will Auceptin, learning that the Aurora Order is searching for people with abnormal luck to find Snake of Fate Will, and discusses creating charms and obtaining placenta blood.
771Klein successfully creates a Luck Siphon charm using the Worm of Time and observes Hazel Macht entering the sewers again under Böklund Street.
772Klein visits the Royal Museum with his butler Walter, who shows interest in William Sikes, a former servant of Prince Edessak, during the exhibition.
773William Sikes is found dead, and Klein follows Walter into the sewers, discovering Trissy who is investigating Prince Edessak's death and learning about Viscount Stratford's involvement.
774Klein interacts with Trissy in the sewers, confirming that Viscount Stratford forced Prince Edessak's suicide, and they agree to cooperate in the investigation.
775Hazel Macht falls sick due to Trissy's ailment powers, and Klein continues his social engagements as Dwayne Dantès while monitoring the situation.
776Klein encounters Leonard Mitchell and Daly at Saint Samuel Cathedral and makes detailed plans with Fors Wall for assassinating Mr. X at the upcoming gathering.
777Klein prepares for the assassination by recording demigod powers in Leymano's Travels, setting up Arrodes to impersonate him as Dwayne Dantès, and disguising as Xio to attend Mr. X's gathering.
778At Mr. X's gathering, Klein and his marionette Senor silently control and assassinate Mr. X using a combination of Marionettist and Wraith abilities, maintaining stealth throughout.
779After the assassination, Klein uses Hurricane and Lightning Storm from Leymano's Travels to create chaos and escapes via spirit world traversal, while Bishop Elektra visits Dwayne Dantès at home.
780Klein returns to his residence, deals with Arrodes's antics after the visit, and above the gray fog, uses Creeping Hunger to graze Mr. X's Beyonder powers, obtaining Traveler's Door.
781Klein examines the Flower of Blood from Mr. X, sets free the Interrogator soul Andy Haydn from Creeping Hunger, and informs Fors Wall of the assassination success.
782Klein deals with the weakness from Death Knell while Alger Wilson advances to Ocean Songster by consuming the potion in an Obninsk's stomach with Mr. Fool's help.
783Klein attends a banquet as Dwayne Dantès and helps Alger communicate with the Obninsk using Mr. Fool's authority to facilitate the advancement.
784At the banquet, Klein socializes with guests and observes Hazel Macht exploring the garden, while discussing ghosts and past adventures.
785Klein sends his marionette Senor to check on Trissy in the sewers, who reveals a hidden passage and plans to leave soon.
786Fors meets her teacher Dorian Gray in Backlund, delivers Mr. X's head, and receives the Scribe potion formula and other rewards.
787Dorian warns Fors about Gehrman Sparrow and gives her properties, while the Church of Storms investigates Mr. X's assassination.
788Klein explores the hidden passage in the sewers but finds nothing, and Emlyn White receives clues about Primordial Moon believers from Ian.
789Emlyn disguises himself and heads to East Borough to hunt the Primordial Moon believers Argos and Galis Kevin.
790In the Tarot Club, Cattleya provides Roselle's diary pages revealing warnings about transmigration and the moon, and Fors starts renting Leymano's Travels.
791Fors explains her rental model for Leymano's Travels, and Emlyn rents it for his hunt, while The World requests a Marauder pathway item.
792The Tarot Club discusses the Scribe potion formula and Leymano's Travels rentals, with Emlyn planning his operation in East Borough.
793Emlyn prepares for his hunt, considering the dangers in East Borough, and Klein receives a visit from Mary Schott about investing in Coim Company.
794Mary Schott proposes that Klein purchase shares in Coim Company to help her retain control, and he agrees to investigate.
795Emlyn successfully kills Galis Kevin using Leymano's Travels but encounters Argos, leading to a battle where Argos loses control.
796Emlyn defeats the mutated Argos, cleans up the scene, and teleports away, then claims his victory at Odora's villa.
797Emlyn receives Lilith's Ring as a reward for winning the hunting competition and plans to use it in the future.
798Klein visits the Coim Company with Mary Schott, meets old acquaintances like Mike Joseph and Aaron Ceres, and considers the investment.
799Klein observes a Keeper in Saint Samuel Cathedral using Spirit Body Threads vision, triggering a near-loss of control and a dream confrontation.
800Klein experiences character dissociation from the Keeper's corruption, drinks holy water, and plans to seek mental purification, while requesting the Mental Terror Candle from Emlyn.
