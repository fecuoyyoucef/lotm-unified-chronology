Ch. 1,001–1,100 Summaries

Lord of the Mysteries

Backlund Aftermath and Intrigues
1001Emlyn White plans the punishment operation against Ernes Boyar at Saint Hierländ Square, with assistance from Leonard Mitchell and Audrey Hall, using hypnosis and mystical items to subdue Ernes.
1002The operation executes as Emlyn and team successfully hypnotize Ernes Boyar, forcing him to follow Emlyn to the Harvest Church where he surrenders his items and agrees to volunteer work.
1003Ernes Boyar is handed over to Father Utravsky at the Harvest Church, while Klein observes the operation and later deals with the aftermath, including splitting spoils among Tarot Club members.
1004Klein detects Amon's avatar in Böklund Street and prepares for battle, with Leonard Mitchell arriving to assist in eliminating the threat.
1005Klein and Amon engage in a tense confrontation, with Klein using marionettes and strategies to stall, while Leonard and Pallez Zoroast prepare to intervene.
1006With help from Will Auceptin's Reboot and Pallez Zoroast's powers, Klein and Leonard successfully eliminate Amon's avatars in Böklund Street, collecting Worms of Time as spoils.
1007Klein enlists Audrey Hall to treat Hazel Macht and others Parasitized by Amon, using hypnosis to help them recover, while planning further actions.
1008Klein and Audrey discuss the mysteries of the supernatural world, and Klein promises her a Fate Siphon charm as payment for her help.
1009Emlyn White advances to Sequence 5 Scarlet Scholar and deals with the Sanguine upper echelons, while Klein attempts to create charms for Will Auceptin.
1010Klein receives a letter from Cattleya about Frank Lee's experiments and confirms a meeting with Queen Mystic, continuing his charm creation efforts.
1011Klein meets Queen Mystic Bernadette, who shares Emperor Roselle's warning about being careful of the 'Spectator' and discusses her own advancement plans.
1012Klein writes to Azik about his life in Backlund, successfully creates the Yesterday Once More charm, and prepares for further charm-making with Worms of Time.
1013Leonard Mitchell visits Tingen City and reflects on past events, while other Tarot Club members like Fors Wall and Derrick Berg make progress in their advancements.
1014Fors Wall advances to Sequence 6 Scribe, Xio Derecha investigates Viscount Stratford, and Emlyn White contacts Marie about operations against the Rose School of Thought.
1015Audrey Hall obtains the Dreamwalker potion formula from Hvin Rambis and is tasked with investigating war attitudes among nobles, while Klein visits Backlund University of Technology with her.
1016Klein receives a letter from Patrick Bryan about the Numinous Episcopate's rituals, and Xio Derecha tails Viscount Stratford, discovering Sherman's transformation.
1017Klein hosts a hunting trip at Maygur Manor with guests like Qonas Kilgor and Framis Cage, while Xio and Fors plan to monitor Viscount Stratford further.
1018Xio and Fors use Psychological Invisibility to spy on Shermane, revealing her identity and connection to Viscount Stratford, but are discovered by Trissy.
1019Trissy sets a trap for Viscount Stratford using Shermane, but he is prepared and survives with Katarina Pelle's help, while Xio and Fors observe from afar.
1020Klein tails Qonas Kilgor to the south bank of the Tussock River, suspecting ulterior motives, while the situation with Viscount Stratford and Shermane escalates.
1021Xio and Fors decide to save Shermane from Viscount Stratford at a warehouse; Fors uses Hurricane from Leymano's Travels to attack Stratford, allowing Xio to intervene.
1022Xio and Fors capture Viscount Stratford using Wintry Blade and other powers; Xio kills Shermane, who is losing control, and they retrieve a bronze cross before escaping with Mr. Fool's help.
1023Xio interrogates Viscount Stratford, learning that Mason Dere discovered King George III's secret and was killed; Stratford explodes after revealing this information.
1024Klein suspects King George III is behind the Great Smog; he prays to the Evernight Goddess and meets Arianna, planning to ambush Qonas Kilgor at Maygur Manor.
1025Klein consults Arrodes about Qonas Kilgor's powers and Sealed Artifacts; Arianna creates a concealed world in Maygur Manor for the ambush.
1026Klein, as Gehrman Sparrow, battles Qonas Kilgor in the concealed world; Kilgor tries to escape but is hindered by the environment and Klein's tactics.
1027Klein uses marionettes and a Deprivation Bullet to fight Kilgor; a Deceit Bullet allows Klein to control Kilgor's Spirit Body Threads, gaining the upper hand.
1028Klein successfully turns Qonas Kilgor into a marionette with a Parasite Bullet; Arianna helps spirit channel Kilgor, revealing information about Ruins No. 1.
1029Arianna and Klein learn from Kilgor about Ruins No. 1, implicating King George III; Arianna plans to inform other Churches and investigate further.
1030Arianna meets with archbishops of the Church of Storms and Steam; they investigate the ruins but find nothing suspicious, as explained by Prince Grove Augustus.
1031Klein discusses with Arianna the possibility that the ruins were 'imagined' by Adam; back at Maygur Manor, he makes preparations and observes the situation.
1032Klein examines the Unshadowed Crucifix above the gray fog, learning it is a relic of the ancient sun god with purification powers and negative effects.
1033Klein divines the origins of the cross, seeing the ancient sun god and experiencing backlash; he gains knowledge of Sun pathway potion formulas.
1034Fors and Xio send a letter to their old address; Audrey learns from Klein about the king's secret and that Hvin Rambis is wanted by the three Churches.
1035At the Tarot Club gathering, Cattleya provides a Roselle diary page; Klein discusses the Unshadowed Crucifix and warns about the king's plot.
1036The Roselle diary reveals the Spectator pathway's godhood ritual; Klein warns Tarot Club members to beware of coincidences related to the king's plot.
1037Alger investigates Feysac colonels for Klein; the Tarot Club members discuss various matters, including the Sanguine operation against the Rose School of Thought.
1038Leonard asks Pallez Zoroast about the Blasphemy Slate; Pallez confirms his great-grandfather saw it and that it was taken by Adam.
1039Derrick tells Colin Iliad about the Creator's relic (Unshadowed Crucifix); Colin hints at exchanging a holy Sealed Artifact for it and discusses the upcoming expedition.
1040Klein meets Audrey at the charity foundation; Danitz checks on Anderson's condition and seeks items related to Vice Admiral Ailment for Klein.
1041Klein meets Sharron and learns that Reinette Tinekerr is her teacher, an angel from the Rose School of Thought's temperance faction. Sharron requests help in an operation against the Rose School of Thought in Backlund.
1042Klein agrees to assist Sharron, and at a Tarot Club mini-gathering, Emlyn explains the Sanguine's plan to target Rose School of Thought members, aiming to retrieve a holy artifact.
1043Klein receives updates from Danitz on Vice Admiral Ailment and Anderson Hood, and Trissy contacts him proposing cooperation against Saintess of White Katarina.
1044Trissy is frightened by Reinette Tinekerr and goes into hiding, while Xio is questioned by MI9 about Viscount Stratford and decides to attract suspicion to involve The World.
1045Xio and Fors prepare for potential investigations, and Audrey is hypnotized by Hvin Rambis to target Xio and Fors, but she resists with Mr. Fool's blessing.
1046Audrey discovers Hvin Rambis's hypnosis and prays to Mr. Fool for help, receiving guidance to treat it as a test of fate.
1047Audrey discusses the situation with Klein and the Tarot Club, planning to misdirect the Psychology Alchemists by implicating the Church of Evernight.
1048The Tarot Club finalizes a plan to deal with Hvin Rambis using Audrey's hat accessory and The World's assistance, aiming to avoid conflict with the Twilight Hermit Order.
1049Audrey meets Hvin Rambis at Viscount Glaint's residence and uses a Fate Siphon charm to hypnotize him, setting up an ambush for later.
1050Audrey successfully hypnotizes Hvin Rambis and summons The World, leading to a confrontation in a concealed world.
1051Klein battles Hvin Rambis using marionettes and abilities, with Hvin Rambis employing Mental Plague and Mythical Creature form defenses.
1052Klein uses Control Spirit Bullets and teamwork with his marionettes to overcome Hvin Rambis's resistance, gaining initial control.
1053Klein and Arianna interrogate Hvin Rambis's soul, learning about King George III's plans and the Twilight Hermit Order's role. Hvin Rambis is killed, and Klein receives the Scholar of Yore potion formula.
1054Arianna helps extract Hvin Rambis's Beyonder characteristic, and Klein makes a deal with Audrey to sell her the Dreamwalker and Hypnotist characteristics.
1055Audrey pays for the Beyonder characteristics, and Klein consults Arrodes about Hounds of Fulgrim and Demonic Wolves of Fog for his advancement.
1056Klein purifies Hvin Rambis's Beyonder characteristic using the Unshadowed Crucifix, creating a mutated bottle artifact, and reflects on staying in Backlund.
1057Klein attends high tea at Macht's residence, discussing investments and the disappearance of Qonas Kilgor, then receives a letter from Sharron about the operation.
1058Emlyn and Marie monitor Charlie Raker as part of the Sanguine operation, but the target is killed by Sharron, triggering a response from other Rose School of Thought members.
1059The Sanguine and Sharron attack a restaurant, but find it empty with a trap involving a crimson moon manifestation, which is neutralized by Duke Olmer.
1060The operation fails, and Klein suspects the Rose School of Thought covered up a secret. Audrey completes payment for Beyonder characteristics, and Klein plans to explore Groselle's Travels.
1061Audrey advances to Sequence 5 Dreamwalker with Mr. Fool's blessing and learns to control dreams. Klein contacts Leonard to help explore dreams in Groselle's Travels.
1062Klein explains the plan to Leonard and Audrey, and they enter Groselle's Travels to explore the dreams of ancient beings.
1063The trio enters Snowman's dream, learning about the Kings of Angels and the ancient sun god's court.
1064They enter Mobet Zoroast's dream, discovering information about the Zoroast family and the Solomon Empire.
1065Mobet shares details about the cosmos and the Apprentice pathway, and they briefly enter Siatas's dream.
1066In Siatas's dream, they learn about elven history, the Queen of Calamity Cohinem, and the deaths of ancient gods.
1067Siatas provides more elven history and traditions, and they explore Frunziar's dream to obtain potion formulas.
1068They enter Groselle's dream, learning about the Giant King's Court and noting illogical details about the Waning Forest.
1069The trio enters the sea of collective subconscious, encountering a giant phantom and using the Unshadowed Crucifix to purify it.
1070They discover the City of Miracles Liveseyd in the sea of collective subconscious and discuss its implications.
1071Inside Liveseyd, they find the Hall of Truth where thoughts are vocalized, and murals that depict the book world and reality.
1072They see murals showing Groselle's Travels and 0-08, and encounter a sealed bronze door that calls to them, forcing a retreat.
1073After escaping, they analyze the sealed door's possibilities and vow to keep secrets, with Audrey using hypnosis to forget details.
1074Klein summons Arrodes to ask about the underground and Groselle's Travels, learning that Arrodes originates from underground.
1075Klein prays to the Evernight Goddess about the underground seal but receives no response, while Fors receives a letter from her teacher.
1076Fors meets Dorian Gray, who gives her the Traveler potion formula and warns about Botis, while Derrick consults with Colin Iliad.
1077Colin Iliad describes four Sealed Artifacts to Derrick, who chooses Life's Cane to exchange for the Unshadowed Crucifix.
1078Derrick exchanges the artifacts, and Fors encounters Botis in Backlund, realizing the danger.
1079Klein uses Life's Cane, which causes rapid plant growth and bizarre effects, and he plans to use it for acting as a Bizarro Sorcerer.
1080Klein continues to experience the effects of Life's Cane and receives a letter from Patrick Bryan about a ritual, which he permits.
1081Klein observes Patrick Bryan's ritual where High Priest Haiter sends a Gatekeeper possessed by the Red Angel evil spirit, Sauron Einhorn Medici, who returns to Backlund.
1082Klein prays to the Evernight Goddess about the Red Angel and receives a cryptic response; Feysac launches an air raid on Backlund, marking the start of the war.
1083Backlund is in chaos due to the air raid; Melissa Moretti rushes to find her brother Benson, while Alger Wilson observes Feysac's fleet movements.
1084Various citizens react to the war; Audrey Hall organizes shelter at Saint Samuel Cathedral, and Klein plans to move to Maygur Manor.
1085Klein analyzes the war situation and is intercepted by the Red Angel evil spirit, who reveals George III's plot to become the Black Emperor.
1086The Red Angel explains the simplicity of George III's plan and offers cooperation in exchange for information on Saintess of White Katarina.
1087The Red Angel discusses the deities' attitudes towards a new Black Emperor, and Klein realizes the connection to Roselle.
1088Klein authorizes Audrey to manage his resources, meets with Saint Anthony Stevenson, and goes into hiding due to Zaratul's presence.
1089Frank Lee plans to advance to Druid with Cattleya's help; Eudora is miraculously healed by Klein's actions.
1090Klein terrorizes the Feysac fleet's demigod Egor Einhorn to digest his potion, and Zaratul's marionette observes the scene.
1091Klein decides to oppose George III's apotheosis and meets Queen Mystic Bernadette to form an alliance.
1092Bernadette shares her failed advancement ritual and agrees to cooperate with Klein against George III.
1093Derrick Berg joins an expedition from the City of Silver with the Unshadowed Crucifix.
1094Xio Derecha receives a reward from MI9 and plans to advance to Sequence 6 Judge with Fors Wall's help.
1095Danitz and Anderson Hood investigate Vice Admiral Ailment's whereabouts and capture the intelligence peddler Bartz.
1096Klein sends Audrey to hypnotize Bartz, and urban legends spread about a 'Clown Angel' healing patients.
1097Klein and Audrey help Lucca Brewster recover from loss of control, and learn about Feynapotter's invasion of Lenburg.
1098Lucca Brewster shares prophecies about the apocalypse in 1368 and thanks Klein and Audrey for their help.
1099Leonard Mitchell leads his Red Gloves team in Backlund as the war expands with Feynapotter's invasion.
1100At the Tarot Club gathering, Cattleya provides a book of Roselle's diary, and Mr. Fool reveals Adam's apotheosis ritual involving the war.
