Ch. 1,401–1,432 Summaries

Lord of the Mysteries

The Fool Ascension Arc
1401Barton and Pacheco return to the foundation after handing a bottle to the Tamara family lady; Vernal appears as a ghost in Barton's study, revealing his corruption and intent to merge with Barton, but Barton wakes from a nightmare.
1402Barton feels relieved as Vernal's matter concludes; he returns to work and meets Alicia Tamara, the new deputy director from the Tamara family, ending the side story.
Modern Day Arc
1403Zhou Mingrui, an ordinary employee, encounters a mysterious vending machine, buys an Assassin beverage, and gains superhuman abilities like enhanced vision and agility.
1404Zhou Mingrui tests his new Assassin powers, recovering from short-sightedness and planning to use them cautiously while dealing with daily work.
1405Zhou Mingrui interacts with Vice President Wu and CEO Huang, who lends him an umbrella and assigns him to pick up a foreign VIP, Mr. Zaratulstra.
1406Zhou Mingrui takes a bus home, senses a ghost, and meets his friend Peng Deng for dinner while contemplating how to use his Assassin abilities.
1407Zhou Mingrui is visited by Officer Deng for a residence permit check and warned about a cult; he later attempts a Leap of Faith and is approached by a police officer.
1408Zhou Mingrui discusses the cult with his brother Benson and sees a warning in the Tarot Club group chat, increasing his vigilance.
1409Zhou Mingrui reads the Tarot Club discussions about the cult and receives private warnings; he considers his options regarding his new abilities.
1410Zhou Mingrui visits a convenience store, is approached by a beautiful woman possibly from the cult, and is warned by Officer Deng again.
1411Zhou Mingrui encounters a ghost in his building, contacts Madam Daly for help, and learns about Detective Sherlock Moriarty's protection.
1412Madam Daly communicates via a translucent face, agrees to exorcise the ghost for a fee, and sends Zhou Mingrui a humorous video; the female ghost watches movies with him.
1413Zhou Mingrui goes to the airport to pick up Mr. Zaratulstra, but the flight is redirected; he meets the chauffeur Rosago and returns to the city.
1414Zhou Mingrui takes a rideshare with a driver wearing a monocle, experiences motion sickness, and arrives at the police station for a project meeting.
1415Zhou Mingrui meets CEO Huang's daughter, Bernie Huang, who asks for help with documents; he later learns he no longer needs to receive Mr. Zaratulstra.
1416Zhou Mingrui takes another rideshare with the monocle-wearing driver, who hints at cult investigations, and arrives at the police station disoriented.
1417Zhou Mingrui buys a notebook and pen from the Star Dream Provisions Store, then attends a meeting with Officer Deng and Old Neil.
1418Zhou Mingrui concludes the project meeting with Officer Deng and Old Neil, who offers exorcism services, and leaves the police station.
1419Zhou Mingrui saves a little girl from an SUV, is thanked by her father who owns a bookstore, and spends time there before heading home.
1420Zhou Mingrui warns the female ghost about Madam Daly's arrival, cancels the exorcism, and later meets Bernie Huang who asks him to register for Dream Tutoring Classes.
1421Zhou Mingrui is hired by Bernie Huang to investigate Dream Tutoring Classes, and he interacts with tutor Dani while discussing business strategies.
1422Zhou enjoys barbecue, meets the lost Bai Ailin, helps him find the hospital, and later finds his notebook tampered with.
1423Zhou guides Bai Ailin to the hospital, discovers Bai disappears mysteriously, and returns home to find strange drawings in his notebook.
1424Zhou learns from the ghost Sharron that an evil spirit tampered with his notebook, and Rozanne offers Instigator and Reporter beverages at work.
1425Zhou experiments with Instigator beverage and dried mushrooms, creating a mushroom man that escapes to Aurora Company.
1426Zhou chases the mushroom man to Aurora Company, witnesses a cult-like gathering, and calls the police after Mr. A eats the mushroom.
1427The police investigate Aurora Company but find nothing suspicious, and Zhou listens to a song by Alger Wilson that causes him to black out.
1428Zhou visits Dream Tutoring Classes, meets Anderson Hood, and signs up for business English classes after recovering from dizziness.
1429Zhou watches a movie about pirates involving potions, is warned of being monitored, and the modern day arc hints at new arrivals.
1430Zhou leaves the cinema, and the chapter concludes the first installment of the Lord of the Mysteries trilogy with teasers for the next book.
1431Treasure hunters Captain Gray, Weimer, Ol' Keaton, and Parvi discover the hidden island of Utopia and explore its eerie, abandoned port.
1432The team explores Utopia's cathedral, experiences terrifying hallucinations, escapes, and Parvi recalls disturbing details about skeletons and a raven.
