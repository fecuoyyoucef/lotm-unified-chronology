Ch. 1,201–1,300 Summaries
Lord of the Mysteries
Forsaken Land and Western Continent
1201
Klein inspects the spoils from Botis, including the Box of the Great Old Ones and a Traveler's Bag, and attempts to bring the Box to the Forsaken Land but fails due to the seal. Leonard Mitchell investigates the Botis battle scene and presents his 'Tarot Club' theory to other church demigods.
1202
Leonard convinces the churches of the Tarot Club's existence, and they decide to monitor rather than confront it. Audrey Hall retrieves the Box of the Great Old Ones and frees Fors Wall and other captured Beyonders.
1203
At a Tarot Club gathering, spoils from the Botis hunt are distributed: Cattleya takes the Secrets Sorcerer characteristic, Fors takes Botis's eyeballs, Xio takes the Traveler's Bag, Leonard takes Worms of Star and gems, Audrey takes a Worm of Star and materials, and Klein keeps the rest. Klein commissions Fors to relay a deal to Dorian Gray Abraham.
1204
Dorian Gray receives Fors's letter about Botis's death and Klein's deal, deciding to risk meeting Klein. Meanwhile, Amon sets a trap using the Jacob family treasure trove, capturing Pallez Zoroast after he absorbs a Sequence 1 characteristic.
1205
Amon attacks Leonard and Pallez in Backlund, using the corpse cathedral to delay divine intervention. Klein responds by using the Box of the Great Old Ones to open the cathedral, but Amon escapes with Pallez's Sequence 1 characteristic, revealing his plan to become a god.
1206
Pallez Zoroast survives by reviving in an avatar at Sequence 2, and Leonard learns that Amon's apotheosis ritual requires replacing someone during their own ritual. Klein realizes Amon's true target was Pallez, not him.
1207
Fors meets Dorian Gray and summons Klein's projection to negotiate. Klein offers to alleviate the Abraham family curse through faith in Mr. Fool in exchange for the Planeswalker potion formula, which Dorian provides.
1208
Dorian tests Klein's promise during a full moon and finds the curse alleviated, moving him to tears. Alger Wilson meets Ralph in Bayam and later retrieves Queen of Calamity Cohinem's inheritance from a coral palace.
1209
Dorian writes to Fors, detailing the two Grade 0 Sealed Artifacts of the Abraham family: the Scroll of God and the Staff of the Stars. Klein considers the Staff more useful for his plans.
1210
Klein encounters a hunting team from Moon City in the Forsaken Land of the Gods, learning about their mutated existence and their guardianship of the grayish-white fog. He shares information about the outside world.
1211
Klein reaches the grayish-white fog barrier and attempts to penetrate it with the Staff of the Stars projection, but fails. He cleanses and heals the Moon City hunters, announcing himself as a missionary for Mr. Fool.
1212
Moon City's High Priest Nim arrives and explains their history guarding the fog under the ancient sun god's orders. Klein purifies the hunters with the Unshadowed Crucifix and Life's Cane, gaining their trust.
1213
Klein learns that Moon City has tried rituals to communicate with the fog, sometimes hearing vague voices. He has Nim perform a ritual, and after a delay, Klein hears voices chanting 'The Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings.'
1214
Audrey Hall struggles with charity work in Backlund due to food shortages and confronts her father about family priorities during the war. Klein attempts to open the fog with the incantation but finds it not yet time, deciding to stay and teach.
1215
Klein realizes the incantation is linked to Sefirah Castle and that the fog may open only at the apocalypse. He tells Moon City residents to wait and begins daily teachings and purification sessions.
1216
In the City of Silver, Colin Iliad prepares for a second expedition to the Giant King's Court, waiting for another elder to advance. Alger Wilson is approved for advancement to Sequence 4 after church assessments.
1217
Klein introduces edible mushrooms to Moon City residents, who are deeply moved by the safe food. Nim gathers the city to share news of Klein and the new hope, leading some to join pilgrimages.
1218
Klein questions Nim about anomalies near the fog and learns of a hill that vanished two years prior, with Dark Demonic Wolf Kotar appearing there. He begins teaching Moon City residents commandments of Mr. Fool.
1219
Cattleya receives an invitation from Bernadette Gustav. Frank Lee decides to aim for Sequence 4 Classical Alchemist. Emlyn White learns he is 12th in line for an Earl characteristic and considers hunting a Rose School of Thought demigod.
1220
Klein investigates the crater where the hill vanished, using divination to see that a 'curtain' artifact was spat out by the fog and taken by Dark Demonic Wolf Kotar. He suspects it is an Attendant of Mysteries characteristic and that Kotar is preparing an advancement ritual.
1221
Klein learns from Moon City priest Duke that the ancient sun god left a message to tell anyone emerging from the grayish-white fog the word 'Chernobyl'. Meanwhile, Audrey Hall manipulates farmers and a grain merchant to obtain mushrooms for food distribution in Backlund.
1222
Klein sends a historical projection to Chernobyl using the Staff of the Stars, finding a research facility with records of an oil field experiment that led to corruption from underground.
1223
Klein encounters an Amon avatar in Chernobyl that reveals information about the Chaos Sea, Blasphemy Slates, and the cosmos (Great Old Ones, Outer Deities), but Klein realizes it's a scam and escapes after learning the secrets.
1224
Klein returns to Sefirah Castle and discusses the cosmos with Audrey Hall, who hypnotizes him to forget the dangerous knowledge. Audrey reflects on the apocalypse and her role in helping Backlund.
1225
Audrey counsels Klein about her dilemmas in manipulating nobles for food, and Klein advises her on taking risks. Emlyn White meets Maric to discuss hunting a Rose School of Thought demigod.
1226
Audrey witnesses the food crisis in Backlund at a soup kitchen, while Klein introduces edible mushrooms to Moon City and gains their conversion to Mr. Fool's faith.
1227
Derrick Berg advances to Sequence 4 Light Guardian with Audrey's help, bringing a brief moment of sunlight to the City of Silver. Audrey discusses her identity struggles with Klein.
1228
At the Tarot Club gathering, Emlyn commissions a hunt for Shaman King Klarman, and Klein seeks collective wisdom on hunting the Dark Demonic Wolf.
1229
The Tarot Club discusses plans for the Shaman King hunt, and Klein considers using prayer to bait the Dark Demonic Wolf.
1230
Klein meets with Emlyn, Sharron, and Reinette Tinekerr to finalize the Shaman King hunt plan, involving historical projections and deception.
1231
Klein consults Will Auceptin and Arianna for advice on hunting the Dark Demonic Wolf, learning to use Sefirah Castle's aura to attract it.
1232
Klein experiments with Sefirah Castle's aura on marionettes, confirming it can affect Beyonders of the Fate pathway. Meanwhile, a battle in Faoltec City involves a Rose School of Thought Shaman King.
1233
Reinette Tinekerr and Klein attack a Shaman King in Faoltec City, but Abomination Suah appears in a trap. Emlyn and Sharron begin their attack on Shaman King Klarman in West Balam.
1234
Klein uses the Box of the Great Old Ones to transport the battlefield to a desert, summoning historical projections of Arianna, Azik, and Will Auceptin to fight Abomination Suah. Emlyn and Sharron battle Klarman, who summons Barranca.
1235
Emlyn uses Lilith's Ring to summon a silvery-white moon that suppresses Klarman's powers, while Sharron curses him. Reinette Tinekerr's main body dispels concealment to assist them.
1236
Sharron and Emlyn kill Shaman King Klarman with an Unshadowed Spear. Klein dispels his historical projection as the operation succeeds, and Abomination Suah is left battling in the desert.
1237
Emlyn receives the spoils from Sharron, including the Shaman King Beyonder characteristic and General of the Pupil-less Eye. Klein purifies the characteristic above the gray fog and seals a drop of corrupt blood.
1238
Emlyn informs the Sanguine of his success, while Cattleya meets Bernadette Gustav on La Cha Island, where Bernadette gives her the Magic Wishing Lamp before setting out to search for Roselle's secret.
1239
Stelyn Sammer resorts to prostitution to get food for her family, while Luke Sammer resists stealing. Audrey Hall bids farewell to her parents, thanking them for her upbringing, as she prepares to take action.
1240
A massive protest over food shortages erupts in Backlund, targeting Empress Borough. Earl Hall and other nobles decide to donate food to appease the masses. Cattleya returns to the Future and reports to Mr. Fool about Bernadette's departure.
1241
Klein communicates with the Genie sealed in the Magic Wishing Lamp through Cattleya's prayer, learning that it knows the former owner of Sefirah Castle and offers a deal for freedom in exchange for the Miracle Invoker characteristic.
1242
Klein cuts off contact with the Genie and begins his plan to hunt the Dark Demonic Wolf Kotar, using prayers to attract it while confusing it with multiple deity invocations.
1243

1244
Klein and Reinette Tinekerr battle the Dark Demonic Wolf in its castle, where it uses the 'Power of Wishes' to suppress godhood, but they overcome it with strategic attacks.
1245

Klein meets the City of Silver expedition team, and they encounter the corrupted Enuni and his shadow, preparing for a fight.

1263
