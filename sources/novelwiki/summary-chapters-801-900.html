Ch. 801–900 Summaries

Lord of the Mysteries

Backlund Resurgence
801Emlyn White borrows the Mental Terror Candle for Klein, who uses it above the gray fog to eliminate a split personality that emerged from psychological stress.
802Klein reflects on resolving his split personality and consults Arrodes about disguising as a Keeper, learning that Queen Mystic Bernadette might help.
803Emlyn receives Lilith's Ring as a reward for winning the hunting competition against Primordial Moon believers, gaining new mystical abilities.
804Klein, as Dwayne Dantès, takes a morning stroll in Backlund, while Audrey Hall discovers an archaeological team tragedy linked to the Psychology Alchemists and a mind dragon.
805Audrey speculates that the archaeological team were Psychology Alchemists members who fell victim to a mind dragon in Hartlarkh Village.
806Klein, as Gehrman Sparrow, meets Alger Wilson on the primitive island, and they begin their exploration using Teleportation.
807Klein and Alger encounter a demigod-level feathered serpent and avoid it by fleeing into another zone of the island.
808The duo finds an Illusory Chime Tree, and Alger kills it to obtain its fruit after using his jarring singing to counteract its hypnotic chimes.
809They encounter a mutated curly-haired baboon (Mentor of Confusion), and Klein quickly kills it using Teleportation, Wraith possession, and Death Knell.
810Klein and Alger enter the ancient ruins, a cathedral of the ancient sun god, and observe murals depicting his greatness.
811Inside the catacombs, they see a picture showing the ancient sun god being consumed by other entities, including figures resembling the Eternal Blazing Sun, Lord of Storms, and God of Knowledge and Wisdom.
812Realizing the significance of the picture, they battle deceased bodies and a six-winged gargoyle, using Lightning Storm and other abilities to prevail.
813They defeat the gargoyle and obtain the Tyrant card from a deceased body, then hear a sigh from deep within the cathedral.
814The primitive island vanishes as they escape; they split the spoils, including the Tyrant card and Beyonder characteristics, before returning to Bayam.
815Klein returns to Backlund, reviews the exploration, and plans for the Tyrant card, considering its implications and potential dangers.
816Fors Wall completes a transaction with Klein, receiving an Interrogator Beyonder characteristic after paying off her debt.
817Klein hosts a ball as Dwayne Dantès, meeting guests including Aaron Ceres and his pregnant wife Wilma Gladys, who carries Will Auceptin.
818Klein dances with Hazel Macht and discusses dreams, prompted by Will Auceptin, who warns him about an angel's interest.
819Fors gives the Interrogator Beyonder characteristic to Xio Derecha as a gift, helping her advancement plans.
820Xio is tailed by Aurora Order members, meets with an MI9 agent, and is tasked with monitoring Viscount Stratford, recalling her father's connection to him.
Foggy Town Imprisonment
821Xio is tasked by MI9 to monitor Viscount Stratford, while Klein sets up a soul imprint in the sewers and attends the Tarot Club, where Roselle's diary reveals information about High-Sequence Beyonder soul imprints.
822In the Tarot Club, Klein shows the Tyrant card and sells Sequence 5 Beyonder characteristics for Druid and Mentor of Confusion, while Cattleya negotiates for a drop of Mythical Creature blood.
823The Tarot Club members complete transactions, with Audrey obtaining the Illusory Chime Tree fruit and Cattleya agreeing to buy the Druid characteristic for Frank Lee, showcasing the club's maturation.
824Audrey shares news of a mind dragon incident, and the club discusses the picture of the ancient sun god being consumed, leading to despair for Derrick about the City of Silver's fate.
825Derrick is advised to make friends, and Klein reserves mental illness treatment with Audrey, while Cattleya confronts Frank Lee about the Druid characteristic.
826Klein explores the sewers but has his thoughts stolen by a demigod, leading to an explosion that destroys evidence, and he divines the theft's danger.
827Klein communicates with Will Auceptin, who agrees to provide a method to regain strength, and later is infiltrated by the demigod's dream, prompting him to plan the notebook theft.
828Klein meets Queen Mystic Bernadette, who uses the Sleep Bugle to contaminate his marionette Senor to mimic Keeper powers for the infiltration.
829Klein prepares for the theft by using a servant disguise and entering Saint Samuel Cathedral, while Audrey plans her Hypnotist advancement with the Hand of Horror glove.
830Klein infiltrates the cathedral, replaces a Keeper, and enters Chanis Gate, setting the stage for stealing the Antigonus family notebook.
831Klein is erased by an angel and sent to the foggy town, where he explores and encounters the mysterious environment and hanging corpses.
832Klein investigates the foggy town, finding it similar to the Hornacis mountain range ruins, and meets Panatiya, who reveals dangers and tricks him.
833Panatiya attacks Klein with her incomplete Mythical Creature form and ailments, but he escapes using Paper Figurine Substitutes as the crimson moon clears.
834Klein hides from the loitering figures and battles Mr. A, using dried mushrooms to calm him, and learns more about the town's dangers.
835Klein enters the ancient cathedral to escape Panatiya, discovering hanging corpses and the statue of a descendant of an ancient god.
836Klein observes the cathedral's interior, noting the Spirit Body Threads being attracted upward, and meets Zaratul, who offers help.
837Zaratul explains the foggy town's secrets, summons Senor from history, and reveals his identity as a high-sequence Seer, providing Klein with enhanced marionette control.
838Klein learns from Zaratul about the Bizarro Sorcerer and Scholar of Yore sequences, and the statue's connection to Antigonus, escaping the cathedral when the crimson moon clears.
839Klein formulates a plan to cooperate with Panatiya, using his marionette to negotiate for the obsidian rock needed to open the escape door.
840Klein and Panatiya discuss cooperation terms, with Klein offering to control her Spirit Body Threads in exchange for the obsidian rock, setting up their joint escape attempt.
Backlund Aftermath
841Klein, through his marionette Senor, negotiates with Panatiya and Mr. A to open the escape door in the foggy town, using Zaratul's provided symbol.
842Klein uses Zaratul's symbol to open the door, but Panatiya betrays him and is hung by the angel, while Mr. A mutates; Klein sees The Fool card.
843Klein battles the mutated mushroom monster formed from Mr. A and Senor, eventually defeating it and obtaining the upgraded Creeping Hunger.
844Klein tries different symbols to escape the foggy town, successfully using the Dark Sacred Emblem to open a door to a mountaintop and return to the real world.
845Klein returns to Backlund as Dwayne Dantès, verifies the Bizarro Sorcerer potion formula above the gray fog, and examines the changes to Creeping Hunger.
846The Nighthawks investigate the Chanis Gate infiltration, suspecting Gehrman Sparrow, and Leonard Mitchell begins to connect clues involving Dwayne Dantès.
847Klein has nightmares from the foggy town trauma and requests psychotherapy from Audrey Hall via Mr. Fool.
848Audrey Hall provides psychotherapy to Klein above the gray fog, helping him overcome his mental issues, and he attends her birthday ball.
849Klein's mental state improves after psychotherapy, and he digests more of the Marionettist potion; Leonard investigates Sherlock Moriarty's connections.
850Leonard discovers that Sherlock Moriarty is Klein Moretti and digs up his grave to confirm; Klein, as Dwayne Dantès, visits the East Balam Military Veterans Mess and is offered an arms deal.
851Klein considers the arms deal offer; Leonard confronts Dwayne Dantès in a dream, learning about The Fool organization and Klein's motives for vengeance.
852Klein warns Leonard about Amon in the Forsaken Land of the Gods; Leonard updates his parasite and decides not to expose Klein.
853Klein confesses to Bishop Elektra about the arms deal, seeking the Church's stance; Daly Simone observes him at the cathedral.
854Klein receives a visit from Baron Syndras, who threatens him over the Coim Company shares, but Klein refuses to sell.
855Walter hires Xio and Fors as bodyguards for Klein; Klein writes to Azik and Frank Lee about Creeping Hunger and mushrooms.
856Police accuse Klein of forcing Cuarón's suicide over the shares; Klein warns Baron Syndras and is bailed out by him.
857Klein analyzes the framing incident, realizing it's a plot against Syndras; Xio and Fors observe Walter's black magic activities.
858Klein donates the Coim Company shares to the Church to establish a bursary foundation, extricating himself from the conflict.
859Audrey Hall advances to Sequence 6 Hypnotist; Klein dines with the Macht family, receives a letter from Frank Lee with new mushrooms.
860During the dinner, a runaway horse and an assassin attack Macht, but are stopped by Fors and Xio; the assassin blames Macht for his factory's bankruptcy.
861Hazel Macht and her family deal with the aftermath of an assault, while Fors has a strange dream about a treasure symbol and discovers Richardson worshiping Death.
862Klein meets with Colonel Calvin to discuss an arms deal and receives a warning letter from Fors and Xio about secrets in his household.
863Klein attends a charity party at Saint Samuel Cathedral, where he meets Audrey Hall and is introduced to Archbishop Saint Anthony.
864Klein interacts with Earl Hall and Audrey Hall, showcasing his acting skills to avoid detection by Spectators.
865Klein gives a speech at the charity party, impressing attendees, and discusses the bursary foundation with Earl Hall.
866The Tarot Club meets; Cattleya provides Roselle's diary pages about 'home,' and The World reveals that Dwayne Dantès is a shared identity.
867In the Tarot Club, transactions occur, and Audrey commissions an investigation on Dwayne Dantès, which The World takes.
868The World explains that Dwayne Dantès is a common identity for him and his companions, shocking Fors.
869The Tarot Club discusses the Hermits of Fate symbol, and Klein plans to deal with the Marauder demigod.
870Klein uses Arrodes to warn the gray rat demigod, who is struck by lightning and decides to leave Böklund Street.
871Klein receives a letter from Sharron requesting help to obtain the Tutanssess II mummy.
872Klein is involved in police investigations regarding Cuarón's suicide, and Leonard Mitchell seeks his advice.
873Klein attends the Loen Charity Bursary Foundation meeting, interacts with Audrey Hall, and discusses operations.
874Leonard visits Klein in a dream to consult on the Cuarón case, and Klein guides him to the truth.
875Klein meets with Sharron and Maric to discuss the plan to steal the Tutanssess II mummy, and learns that Amon is in Backlund.
876Klein devises a plan to divert Amon's attention by informing Pallez Zoroast, and plans the mummy heist.
877Klein, Sharron, and Maric use Arrodes to gather information on the mummy and the trap set by the Loen military.
878Arrodes asks Sharron about her teacher, and they finalize the heist plan based on the gathered intel.
879Klein outlines the heist plan to Sharron and Maric, emphasizing control, risk management, and their roles.
880The heist takes place at Pritz Harbor; Mahmosi and other demigods appear, and Klein initiates the plan by using Hurricane.
881Klein, as Sherlock Moriarty, uses a hurricane to disrupt the demigod battle at Pritz Harbor, steals soil from Tutanssess II's sarcophagus, and escapes with the mummy using Groselle's Travels.
882Sharron follows Klein's plan to create distractions, and Klein successfully retrieves the mummy by smearing blood on Groselle's Travels.
883Klein returns to Backlund, gives the mummy to Sharron and Maric, and receives payment and information on Spirit World Plunderers.
884The Loen military demigods investigate the theft, suspecting Sharron, while Zatwen loses his Sealed Artifact to Amon.
885Klein receives a letter from Sharron with details on Spirit World Plunderers, and Leonard Mitchell plans to leave Backlund to avoid Amon.
886Klein prepares to leave Backlund, visits Audrey Hall at the bursary foundation, and meets Will Auceptin to discuss his birth.
887Klein meets with Colonel Calvin for the arms deal, encounters Brigadier General Qonas Kilgor at the East Balam Military Veterans Mess, and realizes he is a demigod.
888Klein narrowly avoids drawing attention from Qonas Kilgor and discusses with Arrodes about Amon and other matters.
889In the Tarot Club, Mr. Fool warns about Amon's avatar in Backlund, and Cattleya provides Roselle's diary pages.
890The Tarot Club discusses ignoring Amon, and The World shares information on Cuarón's suicide mastermind Hvin Rambis and Qonas Kilgor.
Southern Continent Expedition
891Klein reads Roselle's diary entries about the Twilight Hermit Order and a strange chapel, and Cattleya asks about Roselle's home.
892The Tarot Club transactions occur, and Derrick reports on the City of Silver's former Chief mausoleum with hair-like plants.
893Derrick finds information on Shapeshifter monsters, and Klein leaves Backlund on a military airship.
894Klein meets Daly Simone and Leonard Mitchell on the airship, receives a letter from Azik, and Danitz encounters Anderson Hood in Behrens Harbor.
895Danitz and Anderson struggle with language barriers in West Balam, and Daly probes Klein about his resemblance to an old friend.
896Derrick is summoned by Chief Colin Iliad to help with Worm of Time charms, and he finds a notebook by Colin.
897Colin Iliad performs a ritual to create Fate Siphon charms with The Fool's help, receiving a response.
898Derrick discovers the Shapeshifter information in Colin's notebook, and Klein travels through the Berserk Sea's spirit world to Poto Harbor.
899Klein feeds Creeping Hunger in Poto Harbor and learns about the Black Emperor's crew recruitment after a battle with the Loen warship Pritz.
900Klein observes the Black Emperor's recruitment notice and plans to investigate Azik's copper whistle's abnormality.
