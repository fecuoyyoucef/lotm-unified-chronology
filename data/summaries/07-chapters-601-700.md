Ch. 601–700 Summaries
Lord of the Mysteries
Sea Voyage
601
Klein releases the Psychiatrist's soul from Creeping Hunger, learning about her past and contemplating Groselle's Travels. Darkwill buys a strange die from Mabel's Sundry Store and experiences odd luck.
602
Darkwill discovers a note in the die instructing him to deliver it to Carnot on Oravi Island. News spreads about Gehrman Sparrow killing Mithor King, and Tracy learns of this, suspecting Gehrman Sparrow's involvement.
603
Klein meets Ralph, who tries to convert him to Sea God worship. Darkwill hires Klein as a bodyguard for 1,000 pounds and a request to find a mystical item.
604
Klein and Darkwill board a ship to Oravi Island. Darkwill is struck by lightning due to bad luck from the die, highlighting the dangers they face.
605
Darkwill explains that the die causes bad luck based on its roll. Klein intimidates the Die of Probability using the All-Black Eye, forcing it to cooperate.
606
Klein learns from Will Auceptin about the Die of Probability and how to intimidate it. He successfully scares the die, calming it for 12 hours.
607
Klein uses Sea God powers to calm a storm during the voyage. Fors Wall investigates Williams Street and finds signs of digging in an abandoned chapel.
608
Klein deduces that the evil spirit is using Rafter Pound to attract members of the Einhorn and Sauron families. He reports this to the Churches via the Tarot Club.

610
Klein intimidates the Die of Probability again. A powerful Beyonder appears but is driven away by the die's bad luck effect, set to 1 point.

Audrey helps her dog Susie advance to Psychiatrist. Leonard Mitchell investigates the connection to Sherlock Moriarty in the Capim case. Klein deals with a cheat impersonating him at a bar.
614
Klein meets Bilt Brando, joins the Adventurer Association, and buys Sonia's Golden Spring water for his Nimblewright Master potion.

Further Tarot Club talk about King of Angels. The World obtains footage of Leonard Mitchell from Emlyn to investigate the parasite.
618
Bilt Brando and Sothoth Yann discuss Gehrman Sparrow and a mission. Klein writes to Azik and Will Auceptin for advice on dealing with the parasite in Leonard.

623
