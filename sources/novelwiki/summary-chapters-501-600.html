Ch. 501–600 Summaries

Lord of the Mysteries

Sea Voyage
501White Shark Hamilton speculates about Gehrman Sparrow's true nature and sends a telegram with his theories, hoping to lure pirates, while Klein leaves deliberate flaws as bait.
502Klein receives a letter from Azik recalling scenes from the War of the Four Emperors and warns about the copper whistle; Donna and Denton see his messenger and learn about Beyonder powers.
503Blazing Danitz boards the White Agate in disguise, and Klein captures him, making him stay in a servant's room as a hostage to prevent pirate attacks.
504Red Skull pirates approach the White Agate but flee after seeing Danitz hung out the window, mistaking it for Rear Admiral Iceberg's involvement.
505The group discusses the pirates, and Donna and Denton learn Danitz's true identity and his 3,000-pound bounty, realizing his value as a hostage.
506The White Agate docks at Bansy Harbor; Klein divines danger and decides not to go ashore, while Donna sees a headless zombie outside the Green Lemon Restaurant.
507Captain Elland finds a dead priest in the Storm cathedral; Klein senses increasing danger and goes to rescue Donna's family and others with Danitz.
508Klein and Danitz face headless monsters in Bansy Harbor, using Azik's copper whistle to attract them and fighting their way to the telegraph office.
509Klein rescues Donna's family and others from the Green Lemon Restaurant, then heads to the telegraph office where a woman asks them to find her colleague Paavo Court.
510The group meets the corrupted Bishop Millet; Klein fights and kills him using Creeping Hunger's abilities, including Psychiatrist's Frenzy and Priest of Light powers.
511Klein uses various Beyonder powers to defeat Bishop Millet, while Elland and Danitz deal with the headless monsters using holy water and fire attacks.
512The group returns to the White Agate; Mandated Punishers confirm the situation, but Klein realizes Bansy is Binsy, connected to Rose Redemption and the King of Angels Medici.
513Klein scares Donna and Denton with a distorted appearance to warn them about the dangers and madness associated with Beyonder powers.
514Audrey Hall consumes the Psychiatrist potion and advances to Sequence 7, overcoming the risk of loss of control with help from her dog Susie.
515In the Tarot Club, members trade ingredients and information; Mr. Fool hints about Rose Redemption and the King of Angels Medici related to Bansy Harbor.
516The Tarot Club continues with Emlyn seeking advice on getting support without revealing secrets, and Alger makes guesses about The World's identity as Mr. Fool's adorer.
517The White Agate arrives in Bayam; Klein lets Danitz go, and Donna asks about his reasons for having Beyonder powers, to which he replies 'For my dream. And... protect.'
518Danitz is attacked by Steel Maveti and nearly dies, but Klein saves him using Damage Transfer; Danitz reveals the treasure key and asks Klein to warn his captain.
519Audrey names her mystical item 'Lie'; Danitz recovers and discusses Admiral of Blood's crew with Klein, planning to hunt them for revenge and bounty.
520Klein plans to hunt Admiral of Blood's crew, starting with Steel Maveti, while Alger is tasked with investigating the Death's Key rumors in Bayam.
521Alger Wilson meets Blazing Danitz in a Bayam casino to gather information on Steel Maveti, while Klein enjoys local cuisine and plans his acting by helping dying people.
522Klein investigates the death of adventurer Wendt in Bayam, using spirit channeling to discover he died from Beyonder characteristic corruption, and learns about Wendt's love for Raine on Symeem Island.
523Klein and Danitz discuss their hunt for Steel Maveti, with Klein revealing he will use Danitz as bait. Alger Wilson prays to Mr. Fool, offering assistance in the plan.
524Klein and Alger meet in a divination lodge to coordinate the trap for Steel Maveti using tarot hints. Alger then leaks information to the Mandated Punishers and Calm Squall.
525Alger is detained by Calm Squall but uses Sanguine anesthesia gas to escape. Steel Maveti and his crew observe the ambush at Amyris Avenue, waiting for Blazing Danitz to appear.
526The Mandated Punishers ambush the adventurers at Amyris Avenue using Sealed Artifact 2-37, Dream of Eternity. Steel Maveti's crew then attacks, but Gehrman Sparrow intervenes.
527Gehrman Sparrow quickly kills Steel Maveti using Psychic Piercing and Light of Holiness, while Danitz kills Blood Brambles Hendry. Squall is also killed, and Klein escapes with the Flying Carpet.
528Klein uses Creeping Hunger to Graze Steel Maveti's soul, gaining Zombie powers. The Mandated Punishers investigate the scene, suspecting a Sun believer or Shepherd.
529Danitz returns to the inn and chooses the Flying Carpet as his reward. Klein releases the Nightmare soul from Creeping Hunger, learning about Death's chronicles and Davy Raymond's daughter.
530Klein releases Davy Raymond's soul, who shares his unfulfilled wish regarding his daughter Neelu and information about Death's chronicles linked to Jimmy Necker and Qilangos.
531Klein travels to Symeem Island, disguises himself as Wendt, and confesses to Raine in a dream, fulfilling Wendt's wish. He then sends a death notice to the local sergeant.
532Klein encounters archaeologist Leticia Dolera and her team on Symeem Island. He returns to Bayam and teases Danitz about his increased bounty of 4,200 pounds.
533Danitz hears in a bar that his bounty has risen to 4,200 pounds due to his involvement in killing Steel Maveti. Klein and Danitz prepare to visit the Resistance.
534Vice Admiral Iceberg Edwina Edwards contacts Danitz via dream, learning about Gehrman Sparrow. Klein enters the dream, and they discuss cooperation, with Edwina agreeing to help hunt Admiral of Blood.
535Klein and Edwina exchange information in the dream, covering Beyonder creatures, Death's Key, and the Giant King's Court. Klein proposes hunting Admiral of Blood together.
536Klein and Danitz visit the Resistance in Bayam, meeting Kalat and Edmonton. Klein touches a cursed bone sword, triggering the Sea God Kalvetua's curse.
537Klein and Danitz flee the Resistance area as the Sea God's curse manifests, with followers dying and snakes appearing. Klein returns to the inn to dispel the curse.
538Klein uses the gray fog to dispel Sea God Kalvetua's curse, shattering the bone sword. Kalat and Edmonton discover the sword's destruction and plan to consult their High Priest.
539Klein and Danitz post warnings about Sea God Kalvetua and Leticia Dolera on the Cathedral of Waves. Emlyn White receives a 7,000 pound reward from Nibbs Odora.
540Sea God Kalvetua goes mad, attempting to create a storm and tsunami, but Cardinal Jahn Kottman suppresses it with his Sea King powers, changing the weather and preventing disaster.
541Klein learns about Sea King Jahn Kottman and plans to use Azik's advice to find Kalvetua's hiding place via the spirit world.
542Klein, Danitz, and Elland search for Leticia in Bayam under curfew, encountering pirates and eventually finding her.
543Leticia is killed, revealing she took the 'Book of Calamity' from an ancient elven ruin, not a Sea God ruin.
544Klein decides to sacrifice to Kalvetua to locate its hiding place, claiming expertise in rituals.
545Klein performs the sacrificial ritual, nearly gets killed by Kalvetua, but succeeds in sending an item tainted with gray fog aura.
546Kalvetua dies, and Klein uses the spirit world to find its hiding place, preparing to explore.
547Klein enters the spirit world, encounters spirit world creatures, and finds Kalvetua's ruin.
548Klein fights and kills a corrupted priest in the Sea God ruin, overcoming its madness.
549Klein obtains the Sea God Scepter and a golden wine cup from the elven ruin, then returns above the gray fog.
550Klein studies the Sea God Scepter's powers and negative effects, then responds to prayers from Sea God believers.
551Klein gives new commandments to the believers, and Danitz's bounty increases to 5,500 pounds.
552Klein and Danitz discuss hunting Admiral of Blood using wireless telegraphy, with Klein revealing he has the technology.
553Danitz contacts Vice Admiral Iceberg via Soulfall Ritual, discussing plans to hunt Admiral of Blood.
554Klein acts as Sea God, responding to prayers and helping believers, including saving fishermen from a storm.
555Audrey Hall explores her dreams and the sea of collective subconscious, discovering a mind dragon.
556Arrodes contacts Klein through the radio transceiver, answering his questions about mermaids in exchange for trivial answers.
557Audrey plants a psychological cue on herself to explore dreams, finding trauma and the sea of collective subconscious.
558Klein buries a dead girl who prayed to Sea God, and Jahn Kottman searches for abnormalities in the archipelago.
559Klein explores the spirit world for a messenger but encounters King of the Five Seas, Nast Solomon, and escapes.
560Klein attempts to summon a messenger using a ritual but fails as the spirit world creature is too fast; Alger is fleeced by Ralph for donations.
561Klein attempts multiple summoning rituals to find a messenger, finally succeeding with the headless spirit Reinette Tinekerr, with whom he signs a contract for one gold coin per letter.
562Klein attends a Beyonder gathering with Danitz, where he purchases remnant spirituality of an ancient wraith and learns about a reward for finding red-haired Helene.
563Klein considers using Sea God believers to find Helene; meanwhile, swindler Ed Sheeran is killed by lightning for falsely using The Fool's name.
564Klein saves Admiral of Stars Cattleya from mutation caused by the Hidden Sage and recruits her into the Tarot Club after pulling her above the gray fog.
565Cattleya officially joins the Tarot Club as The Hermit, and Klein discusses her background and the gathering's procedures.
566The Tarot Club meets with new member Cattleya; members trade information and discuss Roselle's diary and other matters.
567Klein reads Roselle's diary entries that reveal the Antigonus notebook's origins and information about the True Creator and subsidiary gods.
568The diary provides details about subsidiary gods in ancient times, such as the Queen of Calamity Cohinem.
569In the Tarot Club, Cattleya provides information on red-haired Helene, linking her to Vice Admiral Ailment Tracy.
570Cattleya guesses that Mr. Fool is the new Sea God responding to believers, and Klein neither confirms nor denies it.
571Klein experiments with dreaming and finds that reciting The Fool's honorific name in ancient Hermes works in dreams; he warns Audrey about dream exploration.
572Klein uses Sea God believers to search for Helene, sending revelations to Kalat and others to find and protect her.
573Danitz fails to buy scalped tickets due to his increased bounty, and Klein helps him by scaring off potential attackers at a bar.
574Klein and Danitz see the Dark Emperor ship captained by Nast Solomon; they plan to meet Vice Admiral Iceberg Edwina in Galagos.
575Klein meets Edwina on the Golden Dream, discussing Tracy, Nast, and cooperation, including assistance to the Resistance.
576Edwina shows Klein her collection, including Groselle's Travels, a mysterious ancient book, and they discuss its origins.
577Edwina explains concepts about Beyonder characteristics and the law of convergence, referencing Emperor Roselle's sayings.
578Klein abducts Helene from the Intis embassy to interrogate her about Vice Admiral Ailment Tracy and her connections.
579Klein asks Helene detailed questions about herself and Tracy, gathering information on Tracy's abilities and activities.
580Klein disguises himself as Helene using Faceless powers to infiltrate Tracy's crew, while the real Helene is sent to safety.
581Klein disguises as Helene and boards the Black Death to assassinate Vice Admiral Ailment Tracy, changing into female attire and gaining insights into Faceless acting principles.
582Klein battles Tracy in her cabin, using various Beyonder powers but fails to kill her quickly; Tracy calls for help, and Klein escapes by jumping into the sea.
583Klein escapes underwater using Sea God powers, reflects on the failed assassination, and plans to contact Azik for help while falling ill from Tracy's ailments.
584Klein contacts Azik via copper whistle, detailing the situation with Tracy and Death chronicles; meanwhile, Mithor King investigates Blazing Danitz in Bayam.
585Klein, ill, visits Darkwill the Apothecary for medicine and accepts a mission to find Roy King; Elland informs about Bansy Harbor's destruction by the Church of Storms.
586Klein takes the medicine and recovers; Danitz leaves with Vice Admiral Iceberg Edwina to aid the Resistance; Azik visits Klein, discussing Tracy and recalling his daughter's grave.
587Azik and Klein travel to the Black Death via spirit world, encounter Tracy and Katarina Pellè; Katarina reveals past connections and agrees to provide Death chronicles.
588Katarina Pellè recognizes Azik as Death Consul from the Pale Disaster; they negotiate, and Tracy demands Helene's location, which Klein refuses.
589Azik and Klein leave; Katarina advises Tracy on pain for advancement; Azik visits his daughter's grave and expresses concern about his past self.
590Klein uses the radio transceiver to contact Arrodes, learning that Roy King is imprisoned in Bayam's governor-general's office and about Groselle's Travels.
591Klein uses Will Auceptin's paper crane to communicate in a dream, receiving clues about finding a mystical item that steals Beyonder powers.
592Klein informs Darkwill about Roy King's imprisonment and receives payment; Darkwill decides to leave Bayam but changes his mind.
593In the Tarot Club, Derrick provides methods to separate mental corruption from Beyonder characteristics; The World commissions Fors to monitor Williams Street.
594The Tarot Club discusses pathways and domains; Cattleya explains the Moon pathway; Audrey reports on the Civil Servant Unified Examination.
595Klein plans to find a mystical item that steals powers, recalling the badge from Lanevus and considering Leonard Mitchell.
596Klein activates the Hermits of Fate badge, learning the next gathering is in June; he divines and plans to contact Arrodes and use Emlyn White.
597Klein receives a letter from Edwina via Reinette Tinekerr, confirming no available mystical item; he uses the paper crane again for clues.
598Klein meets Oz Kent at Amyris Leaf Bar; Mithor King observes him, leading to a confrontation where Klein kills Mithor King.
599Klein uses Dragon’s Might and Distortion to subdue Strongman Ozil and his goons, then kills Ozil and Grazes his soul.
600Klein leaves with the corpses, reports to Oz Kent, and the military decides to continue cooperation while spreading news of Mithor King's death.
