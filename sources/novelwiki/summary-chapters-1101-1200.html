Ch. 1,101–1,200 Summaries

Lord of the Mysteries

Backlund Aftermath and Intrigues
1101At the Tarot Club gathering, Fors commissions Mr. World to help her digest the Scribe potion by teleporting her to unfamiliar places, offering to write horror stories about Backlund hospitals as payment. Emlyn White asks for advice on handling Bishop Utravsky, and Leonard suggests protective custody.
1102Leonard inquires about major matters in Backlund, and Mr. World reveals Zaratul's presence and explains the law of convergence for neighboring pathways. Fors mentions seeing Zaratul's raven marionette watching Botis, and Alger observes the Church of Storms' lack of enthusiasm for the war.
1103Mr. World details neighboring pathways and their conflicts, such as Storm, Sun, Spectator, Reader, and Secrets Suppliant. Derrick reports that the City of Silver expedition is about to enter the Giant King's Court, and Klein advises him to pray to Mr. Fool for luck.
1104Leonard discusses with Pallez Zoroast about Zaratul and the balance of angels in Backlund. Klein reads Roselle's diary entries about the Abyss, the nameless island for his final mausoleum, and his later confusion and extreme plans.
1105Klein continues reading Roselle's diary, learning about corruption from the cosmos and the nameless island's history. He writes to Queen Mystic to investigate the island through the descendants of Roselle's companions.
1106Klein meets Fors and asks her to inquire with her teacher Dorian Gray about Benjamin Abraham and the nameless island. He then teleports Fors to a pirate island in the Berserk Sea for her to record customs.
1107Danitz and Anderson monitor the intelligence peddler Bartz on Theros Island, witnessing Vice Admiral Ailment Tracy's ship, the Black Death. Klein receives their report and teleports to the ship to confront Tracy.
1108Klein captures Tracy on the Black Death, learning she is the child of Demoness of White Katarina and obtaining her blood for divination. He leaves her bound on the ship after setting a 'no disturbance' order.
1109Klein uses Tracy's blood to divine Katarina's location in Backlund and sets a trap with the Red Angel evil spirit. The Red Angel kills Katarina in the mirror world, but she is later revealed to be alive.
1110Klein communicates with Katarina's spirit through a ritual, learning about George III's eight secret mausoleums and the royal family's two angels. The Red Angel evil spirit and Katarina are actually in cahoots, with the Red Angel bluffing about his strength.
1111The Red Angel evil spirit and Katarina discuss their alliance, with the Red Angel revealing his bluffing nature and Katarina's acting. Klein realizes the Red Angel's true target is not Katarina and divines that Tracy has escaped.
1112Klein considers using the Tudor ruins' teleportation nexus to access George III's mausoleums, possibly with Mr. Door's help. Fors writes about her experiences on the pirate island. Derrick and the City of Silver expedition enter the Giant King's Court.
1113The City of Silver expedition faces two Silver Knight guards at the entrance and takes an alternative path through the Waning Forest. They use the Unshadowed Crucifix against evil spirits and enter the Giant King's Court.
1114In the Waning Forest, the expedition battles and defeats the Giant King's remnant will evil spirit, discovering the tombs of the Giant King's parents—human skeletons. They then proceed into the Giant King's Court proper.
1115The expedition explores the Giant King's Court, seeing murals of the God of Dawn and Goddess of Harvest. They enter a hall and hear mysterious footsteps, with Joshua reporting an anomaly.
1116Joshua dies from degeneration on the staircase, and Lovia recites the True Creator's honorific name to dispel the effect. The expedition enters a hall with a long table and murals depicting the Rose Redemption.
1117The hall's murals reveal the Rose Redemption members, including future deities and Kings of Angels. The expedition is trapped in a cycle of fate until Klein helps Derrick break free, and they identify the figures.
1118The expedition continues, defeating a giant statue guard with teamwork. They pass through a hall with illusions of food and approach a door leading to the sea.
1119The expedition opens the door and sees the dark blue sea from the Giant King's Court, fulfilling their long-held hope. However, they are immediately attacked by Light Culler Murskogan.
1120Light Culler Murskogan kills Antiona and attacks the expedition. Colin Iliad releases his Mythical Creature form to fight Murskogan in a fierce battle, while the team retreats towards the sea.
1121Klein observes the battle between Light Culler Murskogan and Colin Iliad in the Giant King's Court, deducing that Murskogan's longevity is tied to living statues, and Colin targets them to gain an advantage.
1122Colin Iliad and Lovia defeat Light Culler Murskogan by destroying the statues; Klein extracts information from Murskogan's spirit about the Silver Knight potion formula and Sasrir's slumber, then advises Derrick to retreat.
1123Derrick returns to the Afternoon Town camp, reports to Colin Iliad, and sees Dolores mourning Antiona; in Backlund, Hazel Macht sees a postman with a monocle (Amon) and has a memory relapse.
1124Hazel is hypnotized by Audrey to forget the incident; Klein teleports Fors back from a pirate island and urges her to write horror stories; he contacts Will Auceptin about accommodating the Uniqueness.
1125Will Auceptin explains the methods for accommodating the Uniqueness and laments missing an opportunity; Klein sends a Yesterday Once More charm to Leonard and prepares to sacrifice Life's Cane to the Evernight Goddess for advancement materials.
1126Klein exchanges Life's Cane for the Demonic Wolf of Fog's transformed heart and White Frost Crystal from the Evernight Goddess; Leonard takes Bishop Utravsky into protective custody, and Emlyn is also detained; Klein attempts to bait Hounds of Fulgrim in the spirit world, but they submit to him instead.
1127The Hounds of Fulgrim offer their eyes to Klein, revealing a connection to Sefirah Castle; Fors begins serializing her horror stories; Klein receives a letter from Trissy proposing cooperation against George III.
1128Klein replies to Trissy, requesting hair from an Abraham family descendant; he receives mushrooms from Frank Lee and prepares for the Tarot Club gathering.
1129At the Tarot Club gathering, Cattleya asks about the Abyss and Roselle's corruption, and Derrick reports on the Giant King's Court expedition, revealing the human skeletons of the Giant King's parents and the members of Rose Redemption.
1130Derrick continues his report, detailing the battle with Light Culler Murskogan and receiving the Silver Knight potion formula; Klein offers Frank Lee's mushrooms to the City of Silver in exchange for the Classical Metallurgist formula.
1131Derrick informs Colin about the mushrooms and receives approval for the exchange; Klein monitors Trissy's ritual through Miss Messenger but loses track due to interference.
1132Klein warns the Evernight Goddess about Trissy's plans; he teleports Fors to a snowy location for her next recording task and receives feedback that his Bizarro Sorcerer potion is nearly digested.
1133Trissy performs a ritual to contact Mr. Door, obtaining the symbol for entering the mausoleums and information on lifting the Abraham family curse; Klein divines the authenticity and prepares for advancement.
1134Mr. Door provides Trissy with the mausoleum entry symbol and the method to lift the Abraham family curse; Klein confirms the information through divination and decides to wait for his potion digestion.
1135Alger returns to the Church of Storms on Pasu Island and is informed of potential advancement; Derrick receives the mushrooms from Klein and tests them with Colin and Lovia in the City of Silver.
1136The mushrooms prove successful in feeding the City of Silver; Klein consults Arrodes about Sefirah Castle and Dark Angel Sasrir, learning about the nine entities left by the original Creator.
1137Klein advances to Scholar of Yore on a barren island in the Berserk Sea, traveling through historical fragments and triggering a reaction from Sefirah Castle that is sensed by Amon, Zaratul, and others.
1138Klein awakens as a Scholar of Yore, examines his new powers including summoning from the Historical Void and enhanced Paper Figurine Substitutes, and establishes an honorific name for Gehrman Sparrow.
1139Klein tests his abilities by summoning a projection of himself and the Master Key from history; Pallez Zoroast explains Sefirah Castle to Leonard, confirming it as the place of Tarot Club gatherings.
1140Klein meets Sharron and Marie to obtain Wraith pathway blood for the mausoleum entry symbol; they discuss plans to restore Reinette Tinekerr's body and oppose George III.
1141Alger Wilson advances to Sequence 5 Ocean Songster on Pasu Island and receives a mission to attack Feysac forces; Audrey Hall learns from her father about King George III's upcoming speech to the kingdom.
1142Klein Moretti gathers Devil blood for George III's ritual but is warned through divination against speaking to Mr. Door; he prepares for the impending confrontation.
1143Klein uses a historical projection of 0-08 to influence events, writing that George III might advance during his speech; citizens including Melissa and Benson Moretti gather for the address.
1144King George III gives a speech using a fake projection while Klein and allies attack his secret mausoleums; Klein summons Arianna for concealment and begins the assault.
1145Klein, Bernadette Gustav, and Trissy simultaneously attack different mausoleums; Klein summons historical projections like Death Consul Azik to create chaos.
1146Intense battles occur in the mausoleums; Klein faces Zaratul and is saved by Will Auceptin using a reboot ability to reset the situation.
1147Trissy sacrifices herself by channeling Primordial Demoness power to destroy a mausoleum, contributing to George III's ritual failure as battles rage with multiple angels.
1148George III's advancement fails, and he dies; Klein escapes the chaos but is captured and parasitized by Amon, who reveals his knowledge of Klein's identity.
1149Amon takes Klein to the Forsaken Land of the Gods, discussing Sefirah Castle and his intentions while transporting through the ruins of the battle of gods.
1150Amon explains the 'Error' pathway as they travel through dream world loopholes, entering the Forsaken Land of Gods via the Giant King's Court.
1151Amon offers Klein a deal to give up Sefirah Castle in exchange for freedom, but Klein refuses repeatedly; Amon parades him while discussing honorific names.
1152They arrive in the Forsaken Land of Gods; Amon removes deep parasitism and gives Klein a chance to escape, beginning their journey through the darkness.
1153Klein and Amon travel through the dream world of the ruins, using a door created by Amon to enter the Forsaken Land directly.
1154In the Forsaken Land, Amon allows Klein to attempt escape without deep parasitism; they walk through darkness towards Amon's destination.
1155Klein rests at a ruined site in the darkness, eating and planning his escape strategies while Amon watches amusedly.
1156Klein makes his first escape attempt by summoning Reinette Tinekerr's projection, but Amon steals it and curses his marionettes with a transformation curse.
1157After a meal, Klein attempts another escape using multiple marionettes to summon help simultaneously, but Amon counters easily, and Klein feigns nonchalance.
1158Klein processes the cursed marionettes, turning them into food, and continues the journey with Amon through a valley with a concealed river.
1159They discuss the City of Silver and other topics; Klein tries to summon help via prayers to Sefirah Castle but is interrupted by Amon's theft of 'day'.
1160In a silent city with phoenix beliefs, Klein uses Mr. Door's items to interfere with Amon's door, but Amon uses 'day' from the ruins to stop him, praising his efforts.
1161Klein and Amon discuss escape attempts, with Amon praising but punishing Klein; they enter a city that worshipped the Mutant King, and Bernadette contacts Klein's messenger, learning he is missing.
1162Bernadette prophecies Klein's danger using clairvoyance; Cattleya, Leonard, and Alger pray to Mr. Fool; Klein and Amon continue their journey, with Amon revealing the Mentor of Deceit ability.
1163Leonard consults Pallez about Klein's situation; Alger plans a military operation on Sonia Island; Klein hears prayers and realizes time is short, making an escape attempt.
1164Klein attempts suicide and summons historical projections of Zaratul and Arianna to fight Amon, but Amon easily counters using errors and time manipulation, stealing the projections.
1165Klein summons multiple angel projections, including Zaratul, Antigonus, Roselle, and a Red of War knight, but Amon uses errors to redirect attacks and steal time, revealing his true body.
1166Klein deduces Amon's scam and confronts him; Amon leads Klein to Chernobyl, revealing it as the ancient sun god's awakening site, and Klein realizes this world is Earth.
1167Klein's connection to Sefirah Castle deepens, but Amon reveals a deeper parasitism and attempts to steal Sefirah Castle, using ravings to disrupt Klein.
1168The Evernight Goddess uses Bladel to obstruct Amon; Klein returns to Sefirah Castle, commits suicide to destroy his body, and reflects on his anchors—the Tarot Club members.
1169Klein analyzes the truth about transmigration and Sefirah Castle, plans to stay in the Forsaken Land of the Gods, and recalls memories of Earth and his loved ones.
1170Klein obtains the Miracle Invoker potion formula through divination, learning the ritual requires returning left-behind history, and considers the City of Silver's escape.
1171Klein lists possible ancient transmigrators, suspecting the Evernight Goddess, and reviews the limited number of Miracle Invoker Beyonder characteristics.
1172At the Tarot Club gathering, Mr. Fool explains George III's failed apotheosis and his game with Amon; members express concern for The World.
1173The Tarot Club discusses ways to help Klein escape Amon, with Cattleya suggesting descent rituals and Leonard offering to research with Pallez.
1174Klein commissions the Tarot Club to hunt the Saint of Secrets Botis for a Worm of Star, with Cattleya, Fors, Xio, and Audrey joining the operation.
1175Leonard consults Pallez, who suggests using a Deceit Charm to deceive resurrection laws; Colin Iliad advances to Silver Knight with Mr. Fool's blessing.
1176Klein uses a Deceit bullet and Sefirah Castle's power to deceive resurrection laws, resurrecting in Derrick's room in the City of Silver, then escapes into the wilderness.
1177Klein prays to deities without response, contacts Will Auceptin through Audrey for information on Miracle Invoker ingredients, and plans to investigate the Nois ruins.
1178Audrey dreams of Will Auceptin, who provides details on Miracle Invoker characteristics and requests ice cream for his Blessed; Klein considers switching to Planeswalker.
1179Fors questions Arrodes about Miracle Invoker ingredients, receiving embarrassing questions; Klein orders Danitz to sacrifice food and prepares for dual advancement paths.
1180Klein tests deity true names in the Forsaken Land of Gods, finding only Leodero's name triggers lightning; Audrey acts as a Dreamwalker, and Emlyn is released from protective custody.
1181Leonard releases Emlyn White from custody, and Klein arrives at Nois Ancient City, using historical projections to explore the ruins abandoned by the Dark Demonic Wolf.
1182Klein encounters the Angel of Holy Word Steph in Nois City, who attacks him using word-based powers; Klein escapes by invoking the Lord of Storms' name, causing lightning to strike.
1183Klein meets Amon in the ruins, who proposes a game to find the Dark Demonic Wolf first; Klein refuses and escapes using careful planning to avoid parasitization.
1184Fors receives a reply from her teacher about the Worm of Star, and Xio considers joining MI9; Klein helps Fors record high-level Beyonder powers above the gray fog to aid her digestion.
1185Alger Wilson explores an elven ruin and encounters Queen of Calamity Cohinem in a dream, who offers him a deal to become a demigod in exchange for delivering an item to the Western Continent.
1186Alger accepts Cohinem's offer, drinking a 'wine' that will lead him to a coral palace for the demigod characteristic, and reports to Mr. Fool above the gray fog.
1187Cattleya arrives in Loen to participate in the Botis hunting operation, and Audrey completely digests her Dreamwalker potion, preparing to advance to Manipulator.
1188Fors advances to Traveler with the help of Klein's messenger Reinette Tinekerr, and Klein uses a historical projection to pray in Backlund cathedrals, encountering a reaction from the Lord of Storms.
1189Audrey advances to Sequence 4 Manipulator during the Winter Gifts Day Mass, using the emotional resonance of the crowd, and gains new abilities like Mind Storm and Mental Plague.
1190At a Tarot Club private gathering, Audrey advises Fors on how to convey Klein's friendliness to the Abraham family, and the group discusses plans to hunt Botis by using Fors as bait.
1191The Tarot Club refines the Botis hunting plan, with Cattleya planting the Eye of Mystery Prying on Fors, Leonard disrupting Aurora Order activities, and Xio providing support with prohibitions.
1192The operation details are finalized: Cattleya and Audrey will lead the attack, while Fors acts as bait and Klein provides remote assistance through prayers.
1193Klein continues searching for the Dark Demonic Wolf in the Forsaken Land, while in Backlund, Fors writes to her teacher Dorian Gray Abraham, and Dorian receives the letter with mixed emotions.
1194Botis and Kisma discuss the Abraham family clues, and Botis uses the Box of the Great Old Ones to capture Fors during a Beyonder gathering, teleporting away with her.
1195Cattleya and Audrey ambush Botis, using magic like Cinderella and Feast of Betrayal; Botis exiles the Box of the Great Old Ones and fights back with recorded abilities.
1196The battle intensifies as Cattleya uses The Ugly Duckling to reveal her Mythical Creature form and Spear of Longinus, while Audrey supports with Mind Deprivation; Botis uses Reboot of Fate to reverse damage.
1197Audrey's Mental Plague triggers in Botis, causing mania and depression; Cattleya and Audrey coordinate attacks, forcing Botis to separate from Kisma and teleport to a predetermined kill box.
1198In the forest outskirts, Audrey uses Mind Storm and Virtual Persona to manipulate Botis, who succumbs to Mental Plague and loses control; Cattleya delivers the final blow with Spear of Longinus.
1199Botis dies, and Cattleya sacrifices the Box of the Great Old Ones and other items to Mr. Fool above the gray fog; Klein cleanses the Beyonder characteristic and seals the dangerous artifact.
1200Official demigods investigate the battle scene, finding only the Hermit tarot card; Klein analyzes the Box of the Great Old Ones, noting its random teleportation abilities and severe negative effects.
