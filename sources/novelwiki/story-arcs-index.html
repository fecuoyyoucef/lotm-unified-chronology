Story Arcs

Major narrative threads

Transmigration and Mystery
Chapters 1–213
Klein Moretti adapts to his new life as a transmigrator, investigates the mysteries surrounding the Antigonus notebook, joins the Nighthawks, and begins his journey as a Seer while dealing with supernatural threats.
Backlund Beginnings
Chapters 214–480
Klein Moretti establishes himself in Backlund, dealing with various mysteries, advancing his abilities, and growing the Tarot Club while confronting threats like the True Creator and corruptions in the City of Silver.
Sea Voyage
Chapters 481–732
Klein Moretti voyages eastward on the Future with Admiral of Stars Cattleya to find mermaids for his advancement, traversing dangerous waters with shared dreams, battling monsters and pirate admirals, and digesting his Faceless potion.
Backlund Resurgence
Chapters 733–820
Klein Moretti returns to Backlund as the tycoon Dwayne Dantès, infiltrating high society to investigate the Great Smog of Backlund and steal the Antigonus family notebook, while dealing with various mystical threats and social intricacies.
Foggy Town Imprisonment
Chapters 821–840
Klein is imprisoned in a mysterious foggy town after being erased, where he explores the eerie environment and faces threats from figures like Panatiya and Mr. A. He meets Zaratul, who explains the town's secrets and teaches him about higher Beyonder sequences, including the Bizarro Sorcerer and Scholar of Yore. Klein formulates a plan to cooperate with Panatiya, using his marionette skills to negotiate for an obsidian rock needed to open an escape door.
Backlund Aftermath
Chapters 841–890
After escaping the foggy town, Klein Moretti deals with the aftermath, including psychotherapy, social intrigues, arms dealing proposals, and a framing incident involving Baron Syndras.
Southern Continent Expedition
Chapters 891–1000
Follows Klein's journey to the Southern Continent, including travel on a military airship, encounters in West Balam, and exploration of the Berserk Sea's spirit world.
Backlund Aftermath and Intrigues
Chapters 1001–1200
Continues the fallout from previous events, focusing on the plot against King George III's Black Emperor ritual, the subsequent battles involving angels and demigods, and Klein Moretti's capture by Amon as he is taken to the Forsaken Land of the Gods.
Forsaken Land and Western Continent
Chapters 1201–1266
Klein Moretti continues his journey through the Forsaken Land of the Gods, hunting the Dark Demonic Wolf and investigating the grayish-white fog barrier believed to seal the Western Continent. He encounters Moon City, spreads the faith of Mr. Fool, and uncovers secrets related to transmigration and ancient deities.
Miracle Invoker Arc
Chapters 1267–1360
Klein Moretti advances to Sequence 2 Miracle Invoker and prepares for his Sequence 1 Attendant of Mysteries ritual by creating the marionette town of Utopia, while dealing with cosmic threats and investigations.
The Fool Ascension Arc
Chapters 1361–1402
Klein Moretti advances to Sequence 0 The Fool, battles Amon in Sefirah Castle, defeats him, and enters slumber to resist the Celestial Worthy's will, while giving commissions to the Tarot Club and setting up future events.
Modern Day Arc
Chapters 1403–1432
Continues the story in the modern world with Zhou Mingrui dealing with supernatural events, cult investigations, and everyday life, culminating in the exploration of Utopia by treasure hunters.
