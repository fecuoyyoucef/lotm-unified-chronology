Ch. 401–500 Summaries

Lord of the Mysteries

Backlund Beginnings
401Mr. A receives a divine epiphany about The Fool's believers in Backlund, while in the City of Silver, Lovia is imprisoned and the curse of killing blood relatives is enforced. Klein relaxes at home but is interrupted by Dr. Aaron.
402Dr. Aaron dreams of Will Auceptin and hires Klein to investigate Grimm Cemetery, where they find a birch tree from the dream.
403Klein and Aaron dig near the birch tree and discover Will Auceptin's腐烂 corpse, leading to police involvement and Nighthawk intervention.
404Fors meets Lawrence Nord at the cemetery, and he entrusts her with a notebook and Beyonder characteristic before dying. Klein divines that Will Auceptin is not completely dead.
405At a Beyonder gathering, Mr. A offers a bounty to find believers of The Fool, alarming Fors and Xio. Audrey attends a psychology meeting with Escalante.
406Klein analyzes the Aurora Order's search for The Fool's believers and decides to lay low. He visits Emlyn White to request help finding Beyonder ingredients.
407Emlyn White agrees to help Klein but reveals his inability to defeat Bishop Utravsky. Fors delivers Lawrence's items to Dorian Gray in Pritz Harbor.
408Dorian Gray uses a crystal ball to verify Fors's honesty and establishes a correspondence with her for mysticism knowledge.
409Klein attends a bike company negotiation with Framis Cage, earning money from shares. Old Kohler informs him about bounties for Azik Eggers.
410Klein visits Isengard Stanton to discuss a case but finds a murder scene with Stanton missing, leading to police interrogation and bail.
411Klein receives threatening letters from the Desire Apostle and reports it to the police. Stuart and other detectives gather at his house after similar threats.
412Isengard Stanton arrives at Klein's house, explains the Desire Apostle's abilities, and they plan to trap him with official Beyonders.
413The Desire Apostle uses emotion control to make Kaslana attack Isengard, but Klein uses Damage Transfer to mitigate the injury, causing the Apostle to flee.
414Isengard reveals he is a Sequence 7 Knowledge Keeper and uses Sealed Artifact 2-081. They discuss the Desire Apostle's abilities and plan further.
415Ikanser Bernard and the Machinery Hivemind arrive, using the Arrodes mirror to identify the Desire Apostle's appearance.
416The team investigates Patrick Jason's house, finding numerous corpses and evidence of his Devil worship, confirming him as the Desire Apostle.
417Audrey Hall is inducted into the Psychology Alchemists by Hilbert Alucard and receives the Telepathist potion, with Mr. Fool's angel providing protection.
418Klein, Isengard, and Kaslana discuss strategies, with Isengard explaining the history of Sealed Artifact 1-42 and their plans to find Jason.
419The detectives share their wishes in case of death, and Ikanser arrives with news that the Desire Apostle has been identified as Patrick Jason.
420The team investigates Jason's house, discovering his atrocities and his identity as a member of the Beria family, but he escapes capture.
421Klein visits the Harvest Church and discusses Sanguine beliefs and Beyonder ingredients with Emlyn White, then plans to use divination to find Jason Beria.
422Klein uses divination to discover Jason Beria's true identity and discusses the Desire Apostle's possible ulterior motives with Isengard Stanton.
423Klein suspects Will Auceptin has been reborn through Dr. Aaron's unborn child, and a mysterious wind suggests Beyonder intervention.
424The Machinery Hivemind uses the Arrodes mirror to locate the Desire Apostle, while Duke Negan heads to his mistress's villa.
425Duke Negan is assassinated by the Desire Apostle, leading to a fierce battle with his guards.
426Leonard Mitchell pursues the Desire Apostle with Sealed Artifact 1-42, and Klein confronts and kills the Desire Apostle in the sewers.
427Klein channels the Desire Apostle's spirit, revealing the Twilight Hermit Order's involvement, and the investigation into Duke Negan's assassination proceeds.
428The official narrative pins Duke Negan's assassination on the Aurora Order, and Klein fully digests his Magician potion.
429Klein plans his advancement to Sequence 6 and learns from Audrey that Mr. Fool's adorer was involved in the assassination.
430Klein receives a reward from the Machinery Hivemind, arranges to buy Beyonder ingredients from Emlyn, and Audrey is informed about the Twilight Hermit Order.
431Klein purchases Beyonder ingredients from Emlyn White, and Fors Wall becomes a student of Dorian Gray from the Abraham family.
432Fors is forced to drink the Apprentice potion again by Dorian Gray, and Klein meets Ian who now deals in information at Bravehearts Bar.
433Klein observes preachers of the original Creator in East Borough and is contacted by Sharron regarding Beyonder ingredients.
434Sharron tells Klein about the dangerous Amon family tomb and a High-Sequence Beyonder's bounty on the Hero Bandit Dark Emperor.
435Klein informs the Machinery Hivemind about the Amon family tomb in exchange for a share of spoils, and Talim Dumont dies from a curse.
436Klein reports Talim Dumont's cursed death to the Machinery Hivemind, but the case is taken over by the royal family.
437Klein discovers that the royal family is investigating Talim Dumont's death, limiting his involvement.
438Prince Edessak Augustus invites Klein to investigate Talim Dumont's death, offering support and rewards.
439Klein meets Prince Edessak at Red Rose Manor, discusses Talim's death, and agrees to investigate with the prince's assistance.
440In the Tarot Club, Audrey Hall provides Roselle diary pages, and Klein learns about Angel Families from the diaries.
441In the Tarot Club, members discuss transactions for Beyonder ingredients and the assassination of Duke Negan, learning about the Twilight Hermit Order from Mr. Fool.
442The club continues sharing knowledge, with Derrick assigned an exploration mission to the Fallen Creator's temple, and Audrey reports political changes in Backlund.
443Klein investigates Talim Dumont's death using divination, encountering interference that hints at high-level power, and sees a mysterious woman at the cemetery.
444Klein meets Prince Edessak, reports lack of progress, and confesses his fears at the Steam Cathedral to avoid deeper involvement.
445Klein investigates Azik Eggers' whereabouts with Old Kohler, then dreams of the Machinery Hivemind exploring the Amon family tomb via Arrodes mirror.
446Arrodes shows the exploration in detail, revealing the Machinery Hivemind's combat style and the discovery of a Human-skinned Shadow.
447The exploration continues, finding a Specter Portrait Frame and encountering a portrait of Amon in the tomb.
448The team finds murals depicting Amon and Adam, learns about the Abyss, and destroys the murals to conceal history.
449Audrey negotiates with the Psychology Alchemists for the Wind-blessed formula and Human-skinned Shadow characteristic, while Klein receives payment from the Machinery Hivemind.
450Klein pays Sharron for her help, and they discuss the baronet digging into the underground ruin, learning about the evil spirit and the Red Priest card.
451Klein successfully concocts and consumes the Faceless potion, advancing to Sequence 6 and gaining shape-shifting abilities.
452Xio receives a new mission from a masked man to find Beyonder ingredients, and Benson decides to move to Backlund after passing the civil servant exam.
453Klein investigates at Red Rose Manor, learning about Prince Edessak's lady and realizing she might be involved in Talim's death.
454Klein cleans his house, reflects on his identity as Zhou Mingrui, and uses divination to suspect Sealed Artifact 0-08's influence.
455Derrick and the City of Silver exploration team enter the Fallen Creator's temple, encountering strange phenomena and a child named Jack.
456Klein and Sharron confront the evil spirit in the ruins, learning about Rose Redemption and the requirements to break the seal.
457The evil spirit provides more information about history, but Klein and Sharron decide not to help it, planning to eliminate it later.
458Emlyn White attends a Sanguine gathering and is tasked by Nibbs Odora to pray to The Fool based on a revelation from the Ancestor.
459Klein misses a meeting with Prince Edessak's lady due to a stomachache, with hints that Sealed Artifact 0-08 might be orchestrating events.
460In the Tarot Club, Derrick reveals that his exploration is stuck in a time cycle, and members discuss possible solutions.
461In the Tarot Club, Derrick shares memories of the Fallen Creator's temple, and members discuss the murals depicting the six gods, with The Fool hinting at Rose Redemption.
462The club analyzes the Rose Redemption text and languages, and Klein reads Roselle's diary entries about miracles and resurrection.
463Klein learns from the diary that a miracle is resurrection from the dead, linking to his own experiences, and the Tarot Club conducts transactions for Beyonder ingredients.
464The Tarot Club warns of danger in Backlund related to Prince Edessak, and Klein advises Emlyn White on praying to The Fool.
465Emlyn White consults Klein about praying to The Fool for help with his psychological cue, and Klein provides statistical advice on the risks.
466Derrick and Colin Iliad break the time cycle in the temple by confronting the boy Jack, revealing connections to the Angel of Fate, Tail-devourer Ouroboros.
467Emlyn performs the ritual to pray to The Fool but receives no initial response, while Derrick escapes the cycle with Colin's help.
468Emlyn is pulled into the gray fog by The Fool, joins the Tarot Club as The Moon, and agrees to keep their deal secret.
469Emlyn shares Sanguine history about Blood Moon Queen Auernia with The Fool, who offers to help in exchange for more historical knowledge.
470Klein visits Red Rose Manor, meets Trissy who reveals she is a Demoness of Pleasure and that her name is Trissy Cheek, linked to the Primordial Demoness.
471Klein realizes the danger of Trissy Cheek and prays to The Fool for help, while Audrey is warned and informs her mother about the threat.
472Audrey tells her mother about being a Beyonder and the danger from Prince Edessak and Trissy Cheek, prompting action from the Church.
473Klein and Azik fall into an underground ruin, where Klein uses Faceless powers to escape, and they encounter Ince Zangwill and an Imperative Mage.
474Trissy and Prince Edessak have a confrontation where she reveals her past and escapes, while he decides to accept his fate.
475Klein overhears Mr. A and Lady Despair planning to use the Great Haze for the True Creator's descent, and he learns about Baelen's involvement.
476Lady Despair creates the Great Haze in Backlund, killing thousands including Old Kohler, Liv, and Freja, while Daisy survives.
477Klein disrupts Mr. A's ritual by throwing the Master Key, causing it to disintegrate and trigger Mr. Door's power, leading to an explosion.
478Lady Despair is killed by a mysterious angel, and Klein continues fleeing from Mr. A, who is severely injured.
479Mr. A is killed by the mysterious angel, who smiles at Klein, and Prince Edessak commits suicide.
480Klein and Azik discuss the aftermath, with Azik giving Klein Creeping Hunger and advising him to leave Backlund, while Klein plans to seek mermaids for advancement.
Sea Voyage
481Klein studies Creeping Hunger above the gray fog, planning to release imprisoned souls, while Audrey learns about the casualties from the Great Smog and Daisy grieves her family's death.
482Emlyn distributes medicine at the Harvest Church and learns his psychological cue was trivial; Audrey comes of age at a New Year's party; Klein visits the cemetery for Old Kohler and Liv, observing new hopeful arrivals in Backlund.
483Klein writes to Azik, sending the Dark Emperor card and a copper whistle, then uses Creeping Hunger to release a Faceless soul for information on mermaids and the Secret Order; the Tarot Club gathering begins with new member Emlyn (The Moon).
484In the Tarot Club, Audrey thanks Mr. Fool for saving Backlund, and members discuss the Great Smog and Twilight Hermit Order; Klein reads Roselle's diary entries about Hermes and the organization.
485Klein continues reading Roselle's diary, learning about Hermes and the Spectator pathway; the Tarot Club discusses Derrick's escape from the time cycle in the City of Silver.
486Derrick recounts his escape from the Angel of Fate's cycle, and the club discusses Kings of Angels; Emlyn asks about improving Sanguine strength, leading to trades and information exchange.
487The Tarot Club members trade Beyonder characteristics and ingredients; Emlyn reveals he is a Sanguine, and Klein (as The World) offers to sell a Faceless characteristic.
488In the Tarot Club, Klein arranges to sell the Faceless characteristic, and Audrey preorders a mystical item; members discuss secret gestures and identity signals.
489Klein decides not to attend a gathering in Babur Valley but divines it, seeing Leonard Mitchell; the Tarot Club ends with discussions on avoiding exposure.
490Klein buys a ticket for the White Agate in Pritz Harbor, meeting a guard who warns about adventures at sea and encounters another adventurer, Cleves.
491Ikanser reports on the investigation of Sherlock Moriarty to the Machinery Hivemind, using the Arrodes mirror; Klein boards the White Agate ship.
492Klein meets former adventurer Cleves on the ship and reflects on Faceless acting principles; they discuss weapons and sea rules.
493Klein joins Cleves, Cecile, and others in hunting a murloc on the deck; Captain Elland helps kill it, and they prepare to cook the meat.
494The group enjoys murloc meat and discusses sea legends and treasures with Captain Elland, including the Fountain of Unaging and Specter Empire.
495Elland continues telling sea treasure legends, such as the Sunken Laurel and Roselle's Treasure, while the group imagines life after finding wealth.
496More treasure legends are shared, and the group reflects on their desires; after the hunt, Klein receives payment from Cleves.
497Derrick returns to the City of Silver, and Emlyn seeks historical information from his parents; Klein continues his voyage on the White Agate.
498Klein visits the Flying Fish & Wine bar in Damir Harbor, encounters scams, and fights Sea Eagle Logan, establishing his Gehrman Sparrow persona.
499Blazing Danitz solicits Klein to join his pirate crew, but Klein intimidates him and learns about White Shark Hamilton's connections.
500Klein interrogates White Shark Hamilton, learning about pirate informant networks and a radio transceiver; he plans to hunt pirates like Admiral of Blood.
