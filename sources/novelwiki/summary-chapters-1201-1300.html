Ch. 1,201–1,300 Summaries

Lord of the Mysteries

Forsaken Land and Western Continent
1201Klein inspects the spoils from Botis, including the Box of the Great Old Ones and a Traveler's Bag, and attempts to bring the Box to the Forsaken Land but fails due to the seal. Leonard Mitchell investigates the Botis battle scene and presents his 'Tarot Club' theory to other church demigods.
1202Leonard convinces the churches of the Tarot Club's existence, and they decide to monitor rather than confront it. Audrey Hall retrieves the Box of the Great Old Ones and frees Fors Wall and other captured Beyonders.
1203At a Tarot Club gathering, spoils from the Botis hunt are distributed: Cattleya takes the Secrets Sorcerer characteristic, Fors takes Botis's eyeballs, Xio takes the Traveler's Bag, Leonard takes Worms of Star and gems, Audrey takes a Worm of Star and materials, and Klein keeps the rest. Klein commissions Fors to relay a deal to Dorian Gray Abraham.
1204Dorian Gray receives Fors's letter about Botis's death and Klein's deal, deciding to risk meeting Klein. Meanwhile, Amon sets a trap using the Jacob family treasure trove, capturing Pallez Zoroast after he absorbs a Sequence 1 characteristic.
1205Amon attacks Leonard and Pallez in Backlund, using the corpse cathedral to delay divine intervention. Klein responds by using the Box of the Great Old Ones to open the cathedral, but Amon escapes with Pallez's Sequence 1 characteristic, revealing his plan to become a god.
1206Pallez Zoroast survives by reviving in an avatar at Sequence 2, and Leonard learns that Amon's apotheosis ritual requires replacing someone during their own ritual. Klein realizes Amon's true target was Pallez, not him.
1207Fors meets Dorian Gray and summons Klein's projection to negotiate. Klein offers to alleviate the Abraham family curse through faith in Mr. Fool in exchange for the Planeswalker potion formula, which Dorian provides.
1208Dorian tests Klein's promise during a full moon and finds the curse alleviated, moving him to tears. Alger Wilson meets Ralph in Bayam and later retrieves Queen of Calamity Cohinem's inheritance from a coral palace.
1209Dorian writes to Fors, detailing the two Grade 0 Sealed Artifacts of the Abraham family: the Scroll of God and the Staff of the Stars. Klein considers the Staff more useful for his plans.
1210Klein encounters a hunting team from Moon City in the Forsaken Land of the Gods, learning about their mutated existence and their guardianship of the grayish-white fog. He shares information about the outside world.
1211Klein reaches the grayish-white fog barrier and attempts to penetrate it with the Staff of the Stars projection, but fails. He cleanses and heals the Moon City hunters, announcing himself as a missionary for Mr. Fool.
1212Moon City's High Priest Nim arrives and explains their history guarding the fog under the ancient sun god's orders. Klein purifies the hunters with the Unshadowed Crucifix and Life's Cane, gaining their trust.
1213Klein learns that Moon City has tried rituals to communicate with the fog, sometimes hearing vague voices. He has Nim perform a ritual, and after a delay, Klein hears voices chanting 'The Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings.'
1214Audrey Hall struggles with charity work in Backlund due to food shortages and confronts her father about family priorities during the war. Klein attempts to open the fog with the incantation but finds it not yet time, deciding to stay and teach.
1215Klein realizes the incantation is linked to Sefirah Castle and that the fog may open only at the apocalypse. He tells Moon City residents to wait and begins daily teachings and purification sessions.
1216In the City of Silver, Colin Iliad prepares for a second expedition to the Giant King's Court, waiting for another elder to advance. Alger Wilson is approved for advancement to Sequence 4 after church assessments.
1217Klein introduces edible mushrooms to Moon City residents, who are deeply moved by the safe food. Nim gathers the city to share news of Klein and the new hope, leading some to join pilgrimages.
1218Klein questions Nim about anomalies near the fog and learns of a hill that vanished two years prior, with Dark Demonic Wolf Kotar appearing there. He begins teaching Moon City residents commandments of Mr. Fool.
1219Cattleya receives an invitation from Bernadette Gustav. Frank Lee decides to aim for Sequence 4 Classical Alchemist. Emlyn White learns he is 12th in line for an Earl characteristic and considers hunting a Rose School of Thought demigod.
1220Klein investigates the crater where the hill vanished, using divination to see that a 'curtain' artifact was spat out by the fog and taken by Dark Demonic Wolf Kotar. He suspects it is an Attendant of Mysteries characteristic and that Kotar is preparing an advancement ritual.
1221Klein learns from Moon City priest Duke that the ancient sun god left a message to tell anyone emerging from the grayish-white fog the word 'Chernobyl'. Meanwhile, Audrey Hall manipulates farmers and a grain merchant to obtain mushrooms for food distribution in Backlund.
1222Klein sends a historical projection to Chernobyl using the Staff of the Stars, finding a research facility with records of an oil field experiment that led to corruption from underground.
1223Klein encounters an Amon avatar in Chernobyl that reveals information about the Chaos Sea, Blasphemy Slates, and the cosmos (Great Old Ones, Outer Deities), but Klein realizes it's a scam and escapes after learning the secrets.
1224Klein returns to Sefirah Castle and discusses the cosmos with Audrey Hall, who hypnotizes him to forget the dangerous knowledge. Audrey reflects on the apocalypse and her role in helping Backlund.
1225Audrey counsels Klein about her dilemmas in manipulating nobles for food, and Klein advises her on taking risks. Emlyn White meets Maric to discuss hunting a Rose School of Thought demigod.
1226Audrey witnesses the food crisis in Backlund at a soup kitchen, while Klein introduces edible mushrooms to Moon City and gains their conversion to Mr. Fool's faith.
1227Derrick Berg advances to Sequence 4 Light Guardian with Audrey's help, bringing a brief moment of sunlight to the City of Silver. Audrey discusses her identity struggles with Klein.
1228At the Tarot Club gathering, Emlyn commissions a hunt for Shaman King Klarman, and Klein seeks collective wisdom on hunting the Dark Demonic Wolf.
1229The Tarot Club discusses plans for the Shaman King hunt, and Klein considers using prayer to bait the Dark Demonic Wolf.
1230Klein meets with Emlyn, Sharron, and Reinette Tinekerr to finalize the Shaman King hunt plan, involving historical projections and deception.
1231Klein consults Will Auceptin and Arianna for advice on hunting the Dark Demonic Wolf, learning to use Sefirah Castle's aura to attract it.
1232Klein experiments with Sefirah Castle's aura on marionettes, confirming it can affect Beyonders of the Fate pathway. Meanwhile, a battle in Faoltec City involves a Rose School of Thought Shaman King.
1233Reinette Tinekerr and Klein attack a Shaman King in Faoltec City, but Abomination Suah appears in a trap. Emlyn and Sharron begin their attack on Shaman King Klarman in West Balam.
1234Klein uses the Box of the Great Old Ones to transport the battlefield to a desert, summoning historical projections of Arianna, Azik, and Will Auceptin to fight Abomination Suah. Emlyn and Sharron battle Klarman, who summons Barranca.
1235Emlyn uses Lilith's Ring to summon a silvery-white moon that suppresses Klarman's powers, while Sharron curses him. Reinette Tinekerr's main body dispels concealment to assist them.
1236Sharron and Emlyn kill Shaman King Klarman with an Unshadowed Spear. Klein dispels his historical projection as the operation succeeds, and Abomination Suah is left battling in the desert.
1237Emlyn receives the spoils from Sharron, including the Shaman King Beyonder characteristic and General of the Pupil-less Eye. Klein purifies the characteristic above the gray fog and seals a drop of corrupt blood.
1238Emlyn informs the Sanguine of his success, while Cattleya meets Bernadette Gustav on La Cha Island, where Bernadette gives her the Magic Wishing Lamp before setting out to search for Roselle's secret.
1239Stelyn Sammer resorts to prostitution to get food for her family, while Luke Sammer resists stealing. Audrey Hall bids farewell to her parents, thanking them for her upbringing, as she prepares to take action.
1240A massive protest over food shortages erupts in Backlund, targeting Empress Borough. Earl Hall and other nobles decide to donate food to appease the masses. Cattleya returns to the Future and reports to Mr. Fool about Bernadette's departure.
1241Klein communicates with the Genie sealed in the Magic Wishing Lamp through Cattleya's prayer, learning that it knows the former owner of Sefirah Castle and offers a deal for freedom in exchange for the Miracle Invoker characteristic.
1242Klein cuts off contact with the Genie and begins his plan to hunt the Dark Demonic Wolf Kotar, using prayers to attract it while confusing it with multiple deity invocations.
1243Klein uses Sefirah Castle to reverse-corrupt the Dark Demonic Wolf, summoning Reinette Tinekerr and engaging in a battle, while Amon watches from the sidelines.
1244Klein and Reinette Tinekerr battle the Dark Demonic Wolf in its castle, where it uses the 'Power of Wishes' to suppress godhood, but they overcome it with strategic attacks.
1245The battle continues with Klein using the Staff of the Stars and summoning lightning via the Lord of Storms' name, eventually forcing the Dark Demonic Wolf to use a resurrection miracle.
1246Klein defeats the Dark Demonic Wolf, obtaining the 'curtain', but is corrupted by the True Creator, who compels him to meet Dark Angel Sasrir in the Giant King's Court.
1247Klein returns to Sefirah Castle, attempts to purify the corruption, and commissions the Tarot Club for assistance, while the Dark Demonic Wolf escapes.
1248Danitz negotiates with Sea King Jahn Kottman for the Resistance's self-governance in the Rorsted Archipelago, and the City of Silver expedition led by Colin Iliad sets off for the Giant King's Court.
1249Audrey Hall leaves Backlund to join the war effort, using her abilities to plant Mental Plague seeds in enemy camps.
1250Klein enters the Giant King's Court, opens the door to the Dark Angel's residence, and his shadow is separated, leading to mutations and battles with corrupted entities.
1251Klein meets the City of Silver expedition team, and they encounter the corrupted Enuni and his shadow, preparing for a fight.
1252Klein and the expedition team battle Silver Knight guards at the entrance of the Giant King's Court, with Klein using Reinette Tinekerr to defeat them.
1253The team enters the Giant King's Court, faces collapsed pathways, and uses abilities to proceed, encountering Enuni and the shadow again.
1254A intense battle ensues where Enuni splits into multiple entities with different pathway abilities, and Klein uses Virtual Persona to resist psychological attacks.
1255Klein tricks the Spectator Saint part of Enuni by using the True Creator's corruption, defeating it, while the City of Silver team holds off other parts.
1256Klein and the team defeat Enuni and the Sun Saint, and Klein battles his shadow, ultimately defeating it and restoring his spirit.
1257The team enters the Dark Angel's slumbering grounds, where Lovia and Colin Iliad mysteriously disappear, and they face the sleeping Sasrir.
1258Audrey battles a Feysac Silver Knight in the war, Anderson Hood faces betrayal in Lenburg, and the war intensifies with deity-level interventions.
1259Klein and the remaining team explore the Dark Angel's palace, encountering darkness that separates them and losing contact with historical projections.
1260Klein meets the sleeping Dark Angel Sasrir, who reveals that the ancient sun god awakened the Primordial One within itself, leading to the assassination by Rose Redemption.
1261Klein and the City of Silver team meet Dark Angel Sasrir, who reveals the truth about the Primordial One's awakening in the ancient sun god and the establishment of Rose Redemption. Sasrir's evil spirit awakens and attacks.
1262The battle against Dark Angel Sasrir intensifies, with Lovia losing control of her body but maintaining her spirit. Klein and the others realize the evil spirit's power comes from Chaos Sea via the first Blasphemy Slate.
1263Klein uses the Staff of the Stars to replicate abilities like time manipulation and dream invasion against Sasrir. Lovia sacrifices herself by merging with the evil spirit, allowing Colin and Derrick to attack.
1264Klein and Colin Iliad target the first Blasphemy Slate to cut off Sasrir's power source. Klein uses 'Theft' to sever the connection, and Colin sacrifices himself in a final attack, leading to Sasrir's defeat.
1265Derrick kills Sasrir with an Unshadowed Spear. Amon appears and steals the first Blasphemy Slate, while the True Creator's corruption leaves Klein's body. Colin Iliad dies after seeing the sunlight outside.
1266Colin Iliad reveals the secret of the 'Gift of the Land' to Derrick and dies. Derrick opens the door to the outside world, and the City of Silver expedition team begins their escape.
Miracle Invoker Arc
1267Derrick leads the City of Silver reconnaissance team out of the Giant King's Court, where they are rescued by Danitz's ship. Klein advances to Sequence 2 Miracle Invoker using the City of Silver's history as a ritual.
1268Klein successfully becomes a Miracle Invoker, gaining new abilities like 'Wishes' and improved historical summoning. He realizes the need for anchors to resist the Primordial One's mental imprint.
1269Klein learns more about the 'Wishes' ability and its connection to collective subconscious. He accesses Sefirah Castle's full powers and recalls sealed memories about the cosmos and Outer Deities.
1270Klein meets the Seven Lights in the spirit world, who provide information about the Outer Deities, the Oldest One, and the Lord of the Mysteries.
1271The Seven Lights detail the Outer Deities like the Mother Goddess of Depravity and Mother Tree of Desire, and reveal that the Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings is the Lord of the Mysteries.
1272Klein returns to Backlund, witnessing post-war recovery. He meets Leonard Mitchell and discusses the apocalypse and the burdens of being a Beyonder.
1273Klein retrieves the magic mirror Arrodes from a Church of Steam train with Bornova Gustav's permission. He writes to Azik about his experiences and plans to wander.
1274Arrodes reveals that Earth Mother is Lilith, the Sanguine Ancestor. Klein learns about the Church of Evernight's post-war strategies and the need for the River of Eternal Darkness for the Goddess's advancement.
1275Klein begins wandering as a magician, fulfilling wishes to digest his potion. The Red Angel evil spirit and Katarina are intercepted by Amon, who proposes cooperation.
1276Klein performs magic tricks in Awwa County, granting wishes like restoring a house and reuniting a girl with her father's spirit, gaining power for his 'Wishes' ability.
1277At the Tarot Club gathering, members discuss the battle of gods and Earth Mother's true identity. Mr. Fool offers higher-level exchanges, including the Sea God's identity.
1278The Hanged Man accepts the mission to cooperate with Sea God believers. The World warns the Tarot Club about the apocalypse arriving in about a decade, urging them to prepare.
1279The Tarot Club members react to the apocalyptic warning. Emlyn visits the Harvest Church and encounters fellow Sanguine who may now worship Earth Mother.
1280Klein arrives in Belltaine City and notices Arianna's presence. He discovers a strange notice from a 'new consul' imposing laws that block his ability to return to Sefirah Castle.
1281Roy, Biles, Phil, and Pasha encounter supernatural incidents in Belltaine City, including a wallet that causes harm and the hanging of Dr. Weber.
1282The group reads a notice imposing laws and is pursued by citizens; Klein appears as a wandering magician, grants wishes, and heals Phil's severed hand.
1283Klein learns from Arianna about Sealed Artifact 0-02, the Trunsoest Brass Book, and the rules tightening in Belltaine City.
1284Klein and the group discuss loopholes in the rules and plan to search for the chief shepherd Larrion in cathedrals.
1285Klein confronts Larrion, who escapes but is trapped by Klein's historical projection; they realize 0-02 is creating a paradox.
1286Klein uses Arrodes to force 0-02 to reveal its name and location, leading to a connection that allows him to find it.
1287Klein finds 0-02 in the library, but Larrion refuses to help seal it, escaping into the spirit world.
1288Klein and Arianna create a paradox to fill 0-02's pages, causing it to reset and erase its rules.
1289Klein seals 0-02 in the foggy town and obtains The Fool card; Larrion ultimately escapes after being trapped.
1290Klein grants final wishes to Roy and others, erases their memories of 0-02, and leaves Belltaine City.
1291Klein studies The Fool card above the gray fog, learning about the Attendant of Mysteries and The Fool advancement rituals.
1292Derrick and the City of Silver reconnaissance team arrive in Bayam and are welcomed by Danitz, preparing for migration.
1293Fors Wall advances to Sequence 4 Secrets Sorcerer with help from Dorian Gray and the Devil's Oil Painting, sealing a demigod creature.
1294During her advancement, Fors converses with Mr. Door, learning about rituals to remove the Abraham family curse.
1295Klein arrives in Constant City, witnessing war aftermath and encountering supernatural cases, including a haunted apartment.
1296Klein helps Red Gloves captain Eric deal with a mutated telescope case that suppresses Beyonder powers.
1297Klein grants wishes to rebuild houses in Constant City, creating a miracle that restores parts of the city.
1298Derrick returns to the City of Silver, and preparations are made for the migration of residents to the new settlement.
1299Klein teleports the City of Silver and Moon City residents to the new settlement on Blue Mountain Island, fulfilling their wish for a new home.
1300Bernadette Gustav arrives at a primitive island, finds corrupted creatures worshiping an empty baldachin, and explores the area with caution.
