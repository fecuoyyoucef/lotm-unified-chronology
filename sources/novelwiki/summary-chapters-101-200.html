Ch. 101–200 Summaries

Lord of the Mysteries

Transmigration and Mystery
101Klein investigates Hanass Vincent's death by showing a portrait to Angelica at the Divination Club and learns from Swain at Evil Dragon Bar that the man was interested in documents about the Hornacis mountain range.
102Klein deduces that the man in the portrait is likely Sirius Arapis and checks borrowing records at Deweyville Library, where he unexpectedly encounters and shoots Sirius after a confrontation.
103Klein searches Sirius's body and finds letters revealing connections to the Aurora Order and the Antigonus family's Seer pathway, then uses a mediumship ritual to learn the Clown potion formula from Sirius's spirit.
104Klein reports the incident to Dunn Smith and the Nighthawks, who confirm Sirius was a Secrets Suppliant of the Aurora Order, and Klein reflects on the implications for his own path as a Seer.
105Klein returns home and cooks dinner for his siblings, then the Nighthawks discuss the Aurora Order case in a routine meeting, where Dunn reminds Klein to control his desires regarding the Clown potion.
106Klein hires Detective Henry to investigate the red chimney and a man at Evil Dragon Bar, while Audrey Hall meets Fors Wall through Viscount Glaint to discuss Beyonder matters.
107Audrey learns that Fors Wall is a Beyonder who can pass through doors and wants help freeing her partner Xio Derecha from jail, and Klein begins his guard duty at Chanis Gate.
108While guarding Chanis Gate, Klein witnesses the Misfortune Cloth Puppet acting abnormally and showing a mysterious symbol, prompting him to call for backup from Leonard and other Nighthawks.
109Leonard deduces that the Antigonus family notebook caused the puppet's behavior, and Klein realizes he forgot to report the symbol, deciding to investigate further using divination above the gray fog.
110Klein uses divination above the gray fog to confirm the symbol is key to the Antigonus family's treasure and reports the incident to Dunn, who informs him the Holy Cathedral has reclassified the notebook as a Grade 1 Sealed Artifact.
111Melissa attends a lecture with friends but leaves due to disagreeable content, while Klein divines that it's unsuitable to go to the Divination Club and returns home.
112Klein visits Azik at Khoy University, who explains his lost memory and strange dreams, revealing that he can see Klein's disharmonious fate and recognizes him as a Beyonder.
113Azik asks Klein to help search for clues about his origin, and during the Tarot Club gathering, Audrey and Alger discuss new members and sequences, with Klein reading a page of Roselle's diary about crude oil.
114In the Tarot Club, Audrey and Alger exchange information about sequences like Apprentice and Arbiter, and Klein sets standards for new members, emphasizing secrecy about his name.
115Klein divines for Glacis and Christina about the swindler Lanevus, offering advice but unable to locate him without personal items, while learning about the reward for his capture.
116Christina returns with her pregnant niece Megose, hoping to use the child for divination, but Megose refuses, and Klein reads about Lanevus in the newspaper, memorizing his appearance.
117Klein contacts Daxter Guderian at Greenhill Mental Asylum, confirming he is a Spectator and member of the Psychology Alchemists, and establishes him as a contact for the Nighthawks.
118Klein's life becomes stable with training and investigations, and he is assigned to work with Leonard and Frye to investigate a series of mysterious deaths in Tingen.
119Klein, Leonard, and Frye investigate Mrs. Lauwis's death in Iron Cross Street, using divination to find supernatural factors and learning about the harsh conditions of the poor.
120The team examines Mrs. Lauwis's body with a mediumship ritual, finding a clue about a slender hand, then visits the workhouse in West Borough to investigate Salus's death, suspecting possible instigation.
121Klein, Leonard, and Frye investigate mysterious deaths; Leonard hypothesizes that life force is being stolen for a ritual, and Klein uses divination to locate the altar.
122Klein's divination reveals a grayish-blue building near a river; they narrow down the location to West Borough and find the target building.
123The Nighthawks attack the building where Trissy is performing a ritual; a battle ensues, and Trissy escapes using substitution and concealment spells.
124The Nighthawks search the building and find evidence of a curse ritual targeting survivors of the Alfalfa tragedy; Klein uses a ritual to see a portrait of Trissy.
125Klein and Leonard deduce that Trissy is Instigator Tris transformed into a female, likely due to a Demoness Sect potion; Klein proposes a plan to bombard the building with cannons.
126Klein divines that Trissy left Tingen by train, realizes his mistake about night trains, and reports the tip; Dunn leads a search but fails to capture Trissy; Klein completely digests the Seer potion.
127Klein feels the effects of the digested potion and continues mysticism lessons; he discusses the acting method with Dunn to explain his rapid progress.
128Klein trains with Gawain and visits the underground market to buy charm materials; he meets Old Neil and Swain, who ask for help with a rampaging Mandated Punisher.
129Klein, Old Neil, and Swain fight the rampaging Mandated Punisher, who has turned into a monster; they kill him, and Swain mourns his former teammate.
130Klein returns home for dinner; Audrey attends a secret gathering in Backlund with Fors Wall and Xio Derecha, meeting Mr. A.
131At the secret gathering, Audrey buys pages of Roselle's diary and seeks potion formulas; Mr. A offers other formulas, but she declines; her dog Susie reports overheard political discussions.
132Klein goes to the underground market to find Ademisaul, who is having nightmares about corpses; he then helps Elizabeth at the Divination Club with her wraith haunting.
133Klein uses a Dream Charm to enter Elizabeth's dream and confirms she is haunted by a wraith; he promises to help and reports to Dunn.
134Dunn, Klein, and Frye go to Lamud Town to investigate the wraith; they use Sealed Artifact 3-0782 to defeat the wraith and discover a hidden basement with a coffin.
135In the basement, they find a headless skeleton and deduce someone recently took the head; Klein sees a portrait of Baron Lamud that resembles Azik.
136Klein is shocked by Azik's resemblance and divines whether to tell Dunn; the divination advises against it; he then notices a prayer from Derrick Berg in the gray fog.
137Derrick Berg, from the City of Silver, prays for help; Klein responds and pulls him into the gray fog, introducing himself as The Fool and offering a chance for exchange.
138Derrick explains the history of the City of Silver and the Giant pathway; Klein names him The Sun and sends him back.
139Klein studies Sealed Artifact 3-0782 above the gray fog and divines its origin, learning it was created by a drop of golden liquid from a sun-like being.
140Klein's divination of the golden liquid nearly kills him and damages the gray fog space; he gains knowledge of Sun pathway potion formulas and the Flaring Sun Charm.
141Klein recovers from using Sealed Artifact 3-0782, meets Frye for shift change, and dreams of Dunn Smith eating his food in a nightmare.
142Klein meets Daxter Guderian to discuss Hood Eugen and the Telepathist formula, reflecting on the 'acting method' and Church secrets.
143Klein ponders the Church's handling of the Seer pathway, divines about obtaining the Clown potion, and meets Derrick Berg in the gray fog for the Tarot Club.
144In the Tarot Club, Audrey, Alger, and Derrick discuss exchanges; Derrick offers the Telepathist formula for the Bard formula.
145Tarot Club continues with Alger sharing info on Mr. A and requesting help to find Rear Admiral Hurricane Qilangos in Backlund.
146The Tarot Club discusses Qilangos's magical item Creeping Hunger and its similarity to Shepherd abilities, with Derrick providing insights.
147Klein meets Detective Henry for a report on the red chimney investigation, and later Daly visits him in a dream.
148Daly teaches Klein a ritualistic magic for summoning a messenger and discusses the 'acting method' and why the Church hides it.
149Klein hints to Dunn Smith about the 'acting method', preparing to submit his special application for advancement.
150Klein and Azik visit Lamud Town, where Azik recalls memories of his past and uses a blood connection to search for a criminal.
151Azik reveals more about his immortal past and agrees to help Klein create a paranormal incident to use Sealed Artifact 3-0782.
152Klein experiments with summoning himself above the gray fog, and Dunn visits his dream again in a silent observation.
153Klein submits his special application to advance to Sequence 8 Clown and shares his experience with other Nighthawks.
154Dunn approves Klein's application after he explains his reasons for advancing quickly due to recent coincidences.
155Klein and Dunn meet Daxter, who provides the Telepathist formula and reports Hood Eugen's abnormal behavior.
156Klein's family hires a maidservant with Elizabeth's help, and Benson discusses civil service exams inspired by Klein's news.
157Fors Wall and Xio Derecha consult detective Isengard Stanton about Qilangos, while Alger interrogates a pirate for information.
158Tarot Club gathering: Derrick provides the Telepathist formula, Audrey pays with Roselle's diary and money, and they discuss Qilangos.
159The Tarot Club continues discussion on Qilangos and the 'acting method', with Klein thinking about bestowing items through rituals.
160Klein, Leonard, and Kenley go to Morse Town to investigate paranormal incidents, taking Sealed Artifact 3-0782 for the mission.
161Klein, Leonard, and Kenley investigate haunting incidents in Morse Town using Sealed Artifact 3-0782, and Klein divines an inverted pyramid mausoleum related to Death.
162Klein secretly uses Sealed Artifact 3-0782 to create Flaring Sun Charms while on duty in Morse Cathedral, experiencing intense sunlight during the ritual.
163Klein becomes suspicious of Old Neil after Rozanne reveals inconsistencies about his marital status, leading Dunn and the Nighthawks to investigate his house.
164The Nighthawks confront Old Neil, who has lost control and transformed into a monster while attempting to resurrect his deceased lover Celeste; Dunn kills him using Sealed Artifact 3-0611.
165Old Neil is buried, and Klein vents his frustrations through training; Dunn informs him that the Holy Cathedral's examiner has arrived.
166High-ranking deacon Crestet Cesimir examines Klein, testing his potion mastery and mental stability, and Klein passes the tests.
167Crestet has Klein swear an oath on the holy artifact and explains the 'acting method,' after which Klein is approved for advancement to Sequence 8.
168Klein concocts and consumes the Clown potion, successfully advancing to Sequence 8 and gaining new Beyonder abilities.
169Klein tests his new Clown abilities, including intuitive prediction and enhanced body control, and discusses the advancement with Dunn.
170Klein visits Azik, who gives him a copper whistle for summoning a messenger, and Azik plans to leave Tingen for Backlund to search for memories.
171Klein's family hires Bella as a maid, and he informs his siblings about his salary increase; Dunn assigns him to help investigate MP Maynard's death.
172Klein uses a mediumship ritual on Maynard's corpse, discovering he died during an affair with Madam Sharon, but the corpse reanimates as a zombie.
173Klein subdues the zombie using charms and ritual purification, and Inspector Tolle identifies Madam Sharon from the vision.
174Madam Sharon admits to the affair, and Klein observes her calm demeanor despite her nervous appearance using Spirit Vision.
175Klein meets Daxter Guderian, who reveals that the swindler Lanevus visited Hood Eugen, linking Lanevus to the Beyonder world.
176Audrey communicates with Xio and Fors via her dog Susie, and Klein attempts the ritual to summon Daly's messenger for the first time.
177Xio and Fors discover Darkholme brutally killed by Qilangos, and they escape using Fors's teleportation bracelet to avoid an ambush.
178In the Tarot Club, Audrey and Alger discuss Qilangos's whereabouts, and Alger requests permission to inform Mr. Fool of findings via ritual.
179The Tarot Club continues discussing Qilangos, and Alger offers to help Audrey obtain the Rainbow Salamander pituitary gland after the mission.
180Klein learns about sacrificial rituals from Derrick Berg and plans to try summoning himself above the gray fog after mastering his Clown powers.
181Klein successfully performs a ritual to summon himself to the gray fog world, experimenting with his spirit state and discovering he can carry items like the Flaring Sun Charm into that space.
182Klein explores Tingen in his spirit state, testing his abilities and limitations, then returns to the gray fog and writes a letter to Azik about Qilangos and sacrificial rituals.
183Klein receives a letter from Daly with detailed instructions on mediumship rituals for living beings, preparing to use it on Hood Eugen.
184Klein and Dunn Smith sneak into Tingen Asylum at dawn to perform a mediumship ritual on Hood Eugen, with Dunn keeping guard.
185Klein conducts the mediumship ritual on Hood Eugen, learning that Lanevus visited him with promises of godhood and help with a ritual, but the ritual is interrupted by a vision of the True Creator, causing Hood to lose control and be killed by Dunn.
186Klein and Dunn clean up the scene and discuss the implications, linking Lanevus to the Aurora Order, while Klein receives a messenger from Azik with a reply.
187Azik's letter warns about sacrificial rituals and provides instructions, and Klein sends another letter about Qilangos's infiltration of Backlund.
188Qilangos, disguised as Baron Gramir, attends Duke Negan's ball with plans to assassinate him, while Audrey Hall detects his disguise and prays to The Fool for help.
189Klein responds to Audrey's prayer and forwards the information to Alger Wilson, then sends a letter to Azik about Qilangos's whereabouts.
190Qilangos attempts to assassinate Duke Negan at the ball but fails due to the Duke's Beyonder abilities and escapes, pursued by Mandated Punishers.
191Qilangos is killed mysteriously by Azik in Backlund, with Alger Wilson and Archbishop Ace Snake discovering his rapidly rotting corpse.
192Audrey learns of Qilangos's death and receives a bounty, while the authorities become concerned about the unknown High-Sequence Beyonder responsible.
193Klein reads about Qilangos's death in the newspaper, and Mrs. Maynard hires the Blackthorn Security Company to investigate Madam Sharon.
194Klein investigates Madam Sharon's routine and decides to infiltrate her house at night, sneaking into her bedroom.
195Klein searches Madam Sharon's room and uses a self-summoning ritual to inspect her safe, finding a white bone statue of the Primordial Demoness and realizing she is a Beyonder.
196Klein reports his findings to Dunn, and they plan an operation with Kenley, using Sealed Artifact 3-0217, to capture Madam Sharon.
197The Nighthawks attack Madam Sharon's house, but she resists using her Witch abilities, and Kenley is killed during the battle.
198Klein and Dunn continue fighting Madam Sharon, with Klein using the Spirit Medium Mirror to reflect her, causing her severe damage.
199Madam Sharon is killed by Klein and Dunn, and Klein performs a mediumship ritual on her spirit to gather information about the Demoness Sect and her motives.
200Klein learns from Madam Sharon's spirit about the Demoness pathway potions and her involvement with Trissy, while Dunn handles the aftermath and Klein notices something strange about Dunn's behavior.
