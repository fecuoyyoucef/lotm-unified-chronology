Ch. 1,301–1,400 Summaries

Lord of the Mysteries

Miracle Invoker Arc
1301Bernadette Gustav explores a primitive island, discovering a cemetery where dead creatures come back to life through a black stone pillar, and encounters Edwards, a knight from Roselle's era.
1302Edwards guides Bernadette to Roselle's mausoleum, where she prophesies that opening it will release a terrifying creature, causing her to bleed from the eyes.
1303Edwards realizes he is dead, triggering anomalies on the island; Bernadette becomes pregnant but uses the Sage Frontlet to transform into information and enter the mausoleum.
1304Inside the mausoleum, Bernadette faces disorders that immobilize her and cause her body parts to come alive; she uses Pale Death to balance her thoughts and sees a black shadow.
1305Klein Moretti intervenes to help Bernadette against the black shadow; Bernadette says 'Home' in Chinese, calming the shadow, which is revealed to be Roselle Gustav.
1306Roselle asks Bernadette to seal him to prevent his corruption; she uses Pale Death with Klein's assistance via grafting, sealing Roselle, and leaves heartbroken.
1307Klein meets Roselle in the mausoleum; they discuss Roselle's corruption by the Mother Goddess of Depravity, the Cards of Blasphemy, and the apocalypse, with Roselle warning about the Mother card.
1308Klein and Roselle say goodbye; Roselle asks Klein to look after Bernadette and shares his regrets before falling into eternal slumber.
1309Bernadette advances to Sequence 2 Sage on the Dawn after sealing Roselle; Orange Light Hilarion informs her about the cosmos under Mr. Fool's orders, while Cattleya has a nightmare about her.
1310Klein warns the Evernight Goddess about the hidden dangers of the Cards of Blasphemy; Emlyn White meets Archbishop Loreto in the Harvest Church and learns about the Favored and Blessed system in the Church of Earth.
1311Alger Wilson is tasked by Mr. Fool to monitor Verdu Garcia Abraham in the Rorsted Archipelago, as part of efforts to control the Abraham family's actions.
1312Klein, as Merlin Hermes, introduces the Fully Automatic Wishing Machine to Jasmine in Limon City, granting her wish to heal her burns from a fire.
1313Jasmine makes two more wishes: for her parents' health and to become extremely beautiful, but the beauty causes problems with men and her parents don't recognize her.
1314Jasmine uses her last wish to cancel her beauty wish, returns to normal, and is approached by Ma'am Grey, who recruits her into the Nighthawks after investigating the incidents.
1315Ma'am Grey summarizes multiple cases of the Fully Automatic Wishing Machine in Limon City, noting its unpredictable appearances and wish-granting with potential distortions.
1316Audrey Hall is contacted by Pauli Derlau, president of the Psychology Alchemists, and enters the mind city Garden of Eden via a carriage driven by an illusion.
1317Audrey becomes a councilor in the Psychology Alchemists, choosing the Pride mask, and learns about the other councilors and her responsibility for the Backlund region.
1318Audrey participates in a council meeting discussing the mind dragon in East Chester County; Derlau explains the Garden of Eden and the name 'Adam' as a life-preserving incantation.
1319Audrey receives information about the mind dragon via mind mail and uses Mr. Fool's help to seal the Pride mask, preventing its influence on her mind.
1320Leonard Mitchell reads about Merlin Hermes and the Fully Automatic Wishing Machine, preparing for a mission; at the Tarot Club, Emlyn asks about the special nature of the Planter and Moon pathways.
1321The Tarot Club discusses the origins of Beyonder pathways, with The Fool revealing that six pathways come from the cosmos: Planter, Moon, Prisoner, Criminal, Lawyer, and Arbiter.
1322Audrey seeks advice on the Psychology Alchemists' request, and Alger suggests stalling for time, while The Fool confirms that Adam has become Sequence 0 Visionary.
1323Derrick asks for economic advice for the City of Silver, and members propose mercenary teams, investments, and other development plans.
1324Cattleya writes to Klein about Frank Lee's potential corruption by the Mother Goddess of Depravity, and Klein divines the risks, advising caution.
1325Over half a year later, Verdu Abraham plans to leave Bayam for Bansy, while Alger monitors him, and the City of Silver proselytizes The Fool.
1326The City of Silver and Moon City residents pray to Mr. Fool to cure deformities, and Amon unexpectedly strikes the bell at The Fool's cathedral, confusing Klein.
1327Klein plans his Sequence 1 ritual, considering three methods for building a marionette town, and chooses a conservative approach on an isolated island.
1328Klein prepares knowledge for the marionettes and creates the town of Utopia on an island in the Berserk Sea, populating it with his marionettes.
1329Alfred Hall's liner stops at Utopia Harbor due to a storm, and passengers disembark, with Tracey soliciting business at the hotel.
1330Wendel, an MI9 agent, stops at Utopia Station due to a storm, witnesses a homicide involving Tracey, and helps her, later discovering Utopia is mentioned in his confidential document.
1331Wendel testifies for Tracey at the police station, comforting her and discussing life, before returning to his hotel where he dries his document.
1332Wendel reads the document and realizes Utopia's anomaly, attempts to escape back to the train, and successfully leaves at dawn, later reporting to MI9.
1333Alfred returns to Backlund, warns Audrey about the dangers of Beyonder potions, and receives an MI9 report on Utopia, linking it to Tracey.
1334Xio assigns the Utopia investigation to her team, and during a blood moon, Fors experiences full moon ravings from Mr. Door, while Emlyn has a disturbing dream.
1335Various perspectives show Klein controlling marionettes in Utopia, Xio investigating, and Verdu heading to Bansy.
1336Alfred and Hibbert travel to East Chester, train dispatchers observe, and Xio's team finds that a Utopia resident, Biles, visited Wendel in Backlund.
1337Biles serves Wendel a subpoena to testify in Utopia, causing Wendel to panic and report to MI9, while Xio coordinates a search.
1338Verdu explores Bansy Harbor, encounters other passengers, uses his classic robe to teleport, and finds a wooden door that reveals a foggy street with a telegraph office.
1339Verdu flees in terror, while Gehrman Sparrow investigates the wooden door, witnessing eerie scenes, and the Red Angel evil spirit observes from Bansy.
1340Monica, a travel columnist, writes about her experiences in Utopia, describing its culture, food, and events, and Audrey hunts with her brothers, discovering they are near Hartlarkh village.
1341Audrey Hall dreams and meets the mind dragon Ariehogg, who questions her about Liveseyd, leading to a tense conversation.
1342Audrey continues her dialogue with Ariehogg, and Mr. Wrath appears, initiating a nightmare battle between the two powerful beings.
1343The nightmare battle between Ariehogg and Mr. Wrath escalates, affecting the manor; Audrey wakes everyone up to prevent corruption.
1344Klein Moretti is attacked in Utopia by Zaratul and historical projections, trapped by Adam in the corpse cathedral after being stabbed.
1345Klein converses with Adam in the corpse cathedral, learning about Adam's identity as part of the ancient sun god and the second Blasphemy Slate.
1346Adam explains the specialness of the Visionary pathway and reveals more secrets about the Great Old Ones and the pillars.
1347Bernadette Gustav prays to Klein via the Magic Wishing Lamp; Verdu Abraham begins a ritual with the God of War to help Mr. Door return.
1348Verdu completes the ritual by sacrificing demigod monsters, preparing for Mr. Door's apotheosis.
1349Mr. Door attempts to return, but Amon steals the ritual and becomes Sequence 0 Error, using the first Blasphemy Slate to block the Mother Goddess of Depravity.
1350Klein uses a wish from the Genie to die and resurrect, escaping Adam's trap and advancing to Sequence 1 Attendant of Mysteries.
1351Klein advances to Attendant of Mysteries while battling Zaratul and dealing with interference from the Lord of Storms.
1352Klein successfully becomes Attendant of Mysteries, defeats Zaratul, and absorbs his Beyonder characteristics.
1353Amon becomes Sequence 0 Error, and Adam goes to meet the True Creator; this marks the end of the seventh volume.
1354After the events, characters like Wendel and Monica find themselves back in their original locations; Fors Wall and the Abraham family receive a influx of Beyonder characteristics.
1355Audrey discovers a dead servant in the manor; Alger Wilson leaves the Church of Storms for the Church of the Sea God after suppressing Sealed Artifacts.
1356Klein stabilizes his condition and seeks advice from Will Auceptin on fooling fate for The Fool's ritual.
1357Klein meets the Evernight Goddess in a dream, learning about Antigonus's ritual and the need for River of Eternal Darkness water.
1358The Evernight Goddess gives Klein an accessory to collect river water; Adam meets the True Creator in the Forsaken Land of the Gods.
1359Klein writes to Azik, reflects on his past in Tingen, and receives a message about Alger; Audrey is approached by Mr. Wrath with choices about her family.
1360Danitz is informed about Alger Wilson's defection, and Mr. Fool appoints Alger as the pontiff of the Church of the Sea God.
The Fool Ascension Arc
1361Alger Wilson is appointed pontiff of the Church of the Sea God by Mr. Fool's revelation, while Leonard Mitchell is tasked with a mission to the Southern Continent against the Rose School of Thought.
1362Klein witnesses Benson Moretti's wedding in Backlund, reflecting on his family and maintaining his humanity.
1363Klein seeks psychological treatment from Audrey Hall to stabilize his humanity against the awakening Celestial Worthy's will.
1364Audrey enters Klein's mind world to awaken his memories, helping him resist corruption and strengthen his humanity.
1365Klein infiltrates the Psychology Alchemists' Garden of Eden, defeats Pauli Derlau, and destroys the mind city under Mr. Fool's orders.
1366Klein offers Xio Derecha the chance to advance to Sequence 4 Imperative Mage in exchange for future assistance, and visits Sharron and Maric.
1367Klein enters Calderón City to retrieve river water from the River of Eternal Darkness, encountering dangers from the undead and ancient beings.
1368Klein successfully obtains the river water after facing Death Salinger and Phoenix Ancestor Gregrace in the distributary of the River of Eternal Darkness.
1369Klein learns from the Evernight Goddess about the seal on the Western Continent and the history of the Lord of the Mysteries and God Almighty's battle.
1370Klein decides to proceed with accommodating The Fool's Uniqueness, rejecting escape options and making final preparations.
1371Klein analyzes potential allies and foes for his upcoming ritual, identifying deities who may support or oppose him.
1372Klein borrows the Magic Wishing Lamp from Bernadette Gustav and negotiates with the Genie for its use in his ritual.
1373Klein arrives at the main peak of the Hornacis mountain range, enters Antigonus's palace, and begins his ritual to accommodate The Fool's Uniqueness.
1374Klein steals Antigonus's identity and fate, facing mental corruption from the Celestial Worthy's will and Antigonus's madness.
1375Amon uses his authority to seal doors worldwide, and Adam becomes Half a Great Old One, trapping three true gods in their divine kingdoms.
1376A divine battle ensues in the astral world as Klein attempts to accommodate The Fool's Uniqueness, with deities like the Evernight Goddess, Earth Mother, and others taking sides.
1377Amon's avatars use ravings to disrupt Klein, but he uses contingencies involving the Magic Wishing Lamp and Trunsoest Brass Book to continue.
1378Klein changes plans, steals his own consciousness to become the main body, and the Lord of the Mysteries awakens in his original body.
1379The Evernight Goddess and others combine forces to suppress the awakening Lord of the Mysteries, allowing Klein to proceed with his advancement.
1380Klein advances to Sequence 0 The Fool after a miraculous resurrection, defeating Amon's interference and completing his ritual.
1381Klein advances to Sequence 0 The Fool and battles Amon in Sefirah Castle, using his new authority of Fooling while dealing with the awakening Celestial Worthy's will.
1382Klein strategizes to escape Sefirah Castle but is blocked by Amon; he creates a divine kingdom of The Fool to stall for time and stabilize his mental state.
1383Klein uses the Trunsoest Brass Book with the Genie's help to set rules against theft and deception, but Amon finds loopholes and continues the battle.
1384Klein conceptualizes himself as a star to attack Amon, who conceptualizes as a door to avoid damage, leading to a clash of authorities.
1385Amon reveals his deceit was to trap Klein, but Klein realizes the truth and awakens the Lord of the Mysteries's will further, forcing a confrontation.
1386Klein forces Amon into a gamble of awakening the Lord of the Mysteries, causing Amon to retreat and ultimately perish in the battle.
1387Amon's main body perishes; Klein absorbs the Beyonder characteristics but faces the Celestial Worthy's awakening, so he seeks the Evernight Goddess's help and enters slumber.
1388Klein holds a last-minute Tarot Club gathering, announcing his slumber and giving commissions to the members to spread his faith and investigate key matters.
1389Klein details the commissions for each Tarot Club member, including spreading his name, investigating pathways, and preparing for the apocalypse.
1390Klein continues assigning commissions, bids farewell to the Tarot Club, writes to Azik, and prepares to enter slumber in Sefirah Castle.
1391Klein returns to Sefirah Castle, releases the transmigrators from the cocoons, and enters the door of light to face the Lord of the Mysteries in slumber.
1392Characters react to Mr. Fool's slumber: Cattleya decides to integrate into the Moses Ascetic Order, Xio and Fors plan, Leonard buys poem anthologies, and Emlyn prepares for the Rose School of Thought besiegement.
1393Alger leaves the Church of the Sea God for a mission with the Book of Calamity, passing leadership to Derrick Berg; Audrey splits her identity and leaves home to focus on her missions.
1394Azik wakes up from slumber and starts a journey to pursue his past; Melissa Moretti performs a ritual with a child, connecting to Klein through a tentacle.
1395Barton, an employee of the Loen Relic Search and Preservation Foundation, receives a letter from archaeologist Vernal about Fourth Epoch ruins and has a nightmare about the pontiff being a golden retriever.
1396Barton investigates Vernal's disappearance with Pacheco Dwayne, finding clues in the Clough Hotel, including a letter with the smell of blood.
1397Barton discovers Vernal visited his home and left a message about the Tamara family, indicating danger from Fourth Epoch refugees.
1398Barton and Pacheco find Vernal in a collapsed house, where Vernal gives them tasks to return a bottle to the suburbs and find a specific lamp.
1399Vernal escapes, and a lady from the Tamara family appears, taking the bottle and revealing her identity, while Pacheco shows signs of Beyonder abilities.
1400The lady from the Tamara family confirms she is from the ancient family and leaves, concluding the side story with Barton and Pacheco.
