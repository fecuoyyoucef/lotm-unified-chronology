Ch. 201–300 Summaries

Lord of the Mysteries

Transmigration and Mystery
201Klein witnesses Dunn Smith kneeling by Kenley's corpse with blood on his mouth, sparking suspicion. He sends letters to Daly and Azik for advice.
202Klein receives no reply from Daly but gets a letter from Azik suggesting Dunn might be a Mutant or have consumed a Vampire potion. He assists Leonard in investigating Lanevus's rented house.
203Klein and Leonard find a letter from Lanevus in the hidden compartment, describing a bomb in Tingen that grows stronger over time.
204Megose unexpectedly visits the Blackthorn Security Company. Klein's spirituality warns him not to look at her, leading him to deduce that the baby in her stomach is the bomb Lanevus mentioned.
205Klein reports the situation to Dunn, who prepares for battle using Saint Selena's ashes. They evacuate non-combatants and plan to confront Megose.
206Klein chooses Sealed Artifact 2-105, the Blood Vessel Thief, for Leonard to use. Dunn retrieves Saint Selena's ashes from Chanis Gate.
207Dunn explains to Klein that he consumes fallen comrades' Beyonder characteristics to keep them with him in dreams, revealing the Law of Beyonder Characteristics Indestructibility.
208The battle against Megose begins. Dunn uses Saint Selena's ashes to restrain her, but she transforms and cries, causing damage. Leonard steals her Blaspheme Curse.
209Klein uses Flaring Sun Charms to damage Megose. Dunn sacrifices his heart to power Saint Selena's ashes, and Megose is killed, but Dunn dies. Klein is then killed by Ince Zangwill.
210Ince Zangwill's notebook reveals he orchestrated the events using Sealed Artifact 0-08 to obtain Saint Selena's ashes and advance to Sequence 4.
211Leonard finds the telegram warning about Ince Zangwill. Klein's funeral is held, and he resurrects from his grave, discovering his ability to come back to life.
212Klein divines that Ince Zangwill and Lanevus are in Backlund. He decides to head there for revenge and opportunities to become powerful.
213Klein bids farewell to Tingen, anonymously gives a flower to his siblings at a circus, and leaves for Backlund, marking the end of the first volume.
Backlund Beginnings
214Klein arrives in Backlund, experiences the polluted air, and takes the metro to Cherwood Borough to find a residence.
215Klein rents 15 Minsk Street from Stelyn Sammer, introducing himself as Sherlock Moriarty, and plans to work as a private detective.
216Klein posts an advertisement for his detective services and prepares for the Tarot Club gathering.
217In the Tarot Club, The Hanged Man provides Roselle diary pages. Klein reads about the War of the Four Emperors and the Laws of Beyonder Characteristics.
218Klein shares the Laws of Beyonder Characteristics Indestructibility and Similar Sequence Beyonder Characteristics Conservation with the Tarot Club members.
219Klein explains the concept of similar Sequences and offers to help with sacrificial rituals for transactions between Justice and The Hanged Man.
220Klein describes the sacrificial ritual to The Fool for The Hanged Man to sacrifice the Rainbow Salamander pituitary gland, and Justice proposes new members for the Tarot Club.
221Alger Wilson successfully performs a sacrificial ritual to send the Rainbow Salamander's pituitary gland to The Fool, and Audrey Hall advances to Sequence 8 Telepathist after receiving it.
222Klein accepts his first detective job from Mrs. Doris to find her cat Brody, and later meets Ian Wright who hires him to investigate the disappearance of Detective Zreal.
223Klein agrees to investigate Zreal's condition for Ian, and they sign a contract with Klein receiving five pounds in advance.
224Klein infiltrates Zreal's house, finds three men lying in ambush, and uses a Slumber Charm to subdue them before performing dream divination to locate Zreal's corpse.
225Klein takes Ian to see Zreal's body in the sewers, and Ian reveals he owes Klein three favors, directing him to Kaspars for a gun.
226Xio discovers an incantation in a book that attracts The Fool's attention, causing her fear, and Klein experiences a warm current on his hand.
227Meursault visits Klein to hire him to find Ian, but Klein refuses; later, Klein visits inventor Leppard and invests in a bicycle project.
228Meursault attacks Klein at home, but Klein kills him in self-defense and uses mediumship to learn that Ambassador Bakerland is behind the pursuit of Ian.
229Klein decides to report the incident to the police, pays bail with Jurgen's help, and is interrogated by a Beyonder from the military's special department.
230Klein is released on bail, discusses compensation with his landlords, and Xio undergoes a purification ritual for her suspected haunting.
231Klein returns home to clean up the mess, plans to hire a bodyguard, and Xio purchases the Sheriff potion formula at a Beyonder gathering.
232Klein visits Bravehearts Bar to buy a gun from Kaspars and meets Maric, a Beyonder who plays cards with zombies, but fails to hire him as a bodyguard.
233Klein uses divination to find Ian's whereabouts and experiences the warm current again, realizing it's connected to Xio's incantation.
234Klein discovers that an iron-black threadworm infiltrated his room to read the information on Ian, and he attends the Tarot Club gathering.
235Klein has breakfast with Jurgen, signs an investment contract with Leppard, and reads about Ambassador Bakerland in the library.
236At the Tarot Club, Mr. Fool issues a mission to assassinate Ambassador Bakerland, which Audrey and Alger accept in exchange for rewards.
237Klein reads Roselle's diary entries about Sequence 2 Miracle Invoker and Beyonder characteristics inheritance, and the Tarot Club members trade information.
238Derrick shares information on dragons, including the Dragon of Imagination Ankewelt, and Audrey receives a telegram about Rainbow Salamanders from her brother.
239Audrey plans to gather information on Bakerland at a ball, Alger attends a secret meeting, and Klein prepares to visit Bravehearts Bar again.
240Klein attends a Beyonder gathering where he sells Meursault's Beyonder characteristic and buys a mystical voice item from Black Snake.
241Klein attends a Beyonder gathering and buys a black ear from Black Snake, later using it above the gray fog to create the Language of Foulness charm after enduring the True Creator's voice.
242Klein takes on a detective job from Mary Gale to investigate her husband's affair, while Audrey Hall gathers information on Ambassador Bakerland at a noble ball.
243Klein confirms Doragu Gale's affair at the Quelaag Club, and Audrey, with Xio and Fors, contacts Mr. A to assassinate Bakerland for 10,000 pounds.
244Mr. A agrees to the assassination mission, and Klein hires Maric's friend as a bodyguard for 1,000 pounds after divining the risks.
245Klein receives money from Justice via prayer, joins the Quelaag Club as a member, and divines about the impending danger.
246Klein experiences a strange omen of danger, hires the bodyguard (a mysterious woman in black), and sees her appear in a mirror at his home.
247Ian Wright visits Klein, explaining the full story about the Helmosuin manuscript and spy conspiracy, leading Klein to reflect on his mistakes.
248Klein prepares for an attack as Bakerland is assassinated by Mr. A in his embassy, using Shepherd abilities to kill him.
249The detailed assassination of Bakerland by Mr. A, who uses Rose Bishop and Shepherd powers to overcome Bakerland's defenses.
250Rosago, disguised as Sergeant Faxine, attacks Klein at home, but Klein uses the Language of Foulness charm with his bodyguard's help to kill Rosago after a fierce battle.
251Klein performs mediumship on Rosago above the gray fog, obtaining the Seer pathway potion formulas (Magician, Faceless, Nimblewright Master), and divides the spoils with his bodyguard.
252Miss Bodyguard cleans up the murder scene, and Klein confirms Bakerland's death via divination, ending the immediate threat.
253Klein reflects on the coincidences leading to his Seer pathway gains and plans for the future, while Miss Bodyguard warns him about MI9's visit.
254Audrey learns of Bakerland's death from the newspapers, and Klein accepts a new commission from Millet Carter to explore an underground structure.
255Klein successfully photographs Doragu Gale's affair, gets paid by Mary, and discusses mystical items with the Apothecary and Eye of Wisdom at a gathering.
256Klein attends another Beyonder gathering, learns of Black Snake's death in the sewers, and hears about Sealed Artifact 2-081 from Eye of Wisdom.
257Klein learns more about Sealed Artifacts and Eye of Wisdom's collection, including the effects and flaws of various mystical items.
258On the way home, Klein witnesses a murder case likely committed by the Blood Sanctify Sect, and later accepts Millet Carter's exploration commission.
259Klein explores the underground structure with Miss Bodyguard, encounters a snake nest, and suspects the structure is a relic from the Fourth Epoch.
260Klein finds a hall with two thrones, one bearing the Tudor family emblem, and discovers a statue resembling the Evernight Goddess, deepening the mystery.
261Klein and Miss Bodyguard explore an underground structure with statues of six deities and encounter an evil spirit near a bloody door, escaping with Azik's copper whistle's help.
262They decide to investigate the Pound family, and Klein has a dream about a dragon and a knight linked to the bloody door, influenced by the evil spirit's aura.
263Klein experiences spiritual perception from the aura and experiments with Azik's copper whistle and the All-Black Eye above the gray fog, creating a fake Tarot Club member.
264Klein perfects his smurf identity 'The World' for the Tarot Club and prepares for the next gathering.
265In the Tarot Club, new member The World is introduced, and Klein reads Roselle's diary about Cards of Blasphemy and the existence of Sequence 0 True Gods.
266The Tarot Club discusses commissions; The World requests information on abject nobles and trades knowledge with Justice about the Demoness pathway.
267The World pays Justice with details on Witch and Pleasure sequences, and Klein learns about the Sauron family from Roselle's diary.
268Klein tests Azik's copper whistle at Aston Cemetery and St. Estin's Hospital, confirming it only affects unritualized corpses.
269Audrey hires Xio and Fors to investigate Lanevus, and Klein meets reporter Mike Joseph, who hires him for protection at the Golden Rose.
270Klein protects Mike at the Golden Rose brothel, learning about the serial killings and connections to Capim from Ma'am Lopez.
271They infiltrate Lopez's room, hear about Capim, and are approached by detective Isengard Stanton for information.
272Klein visits Bravehearts Bar, suspects Miss Bodyguard is being hunted, and learns about a Beyonder gathering from Kaspars.
273At the Beyonder gathering, Klein sells Bard and Light Suppliant potion formulas and buys the Axe of Hurricane for The Sun.
274Klein sacrifices the Axe of Hurricane to The Sun above the gray fog and plans to investigate the Pound family further.
275Klein obtains information on the Pound family from Justice and plans to visit Rafter Pound in Sivellaus Street.
276Klein visits Rafter Pound in disguise, using dream divination and spirit channeling but suspects deception and a connection to the Tudor family.
277Klein divines Rafter Pound's future, revealing a link to the Tudor family via a black iron emblem, and finalizes a plan to collapse the underground structure.
278Klein hires Miss Bodyguard Sharron for demolition, and Xio discovers that informants Williams and Gavin have been killed in the Lanevus investigation.
279Sharron demolishes the entrance to the underground structure, sealing it, and Klein is arrested for illegal gun possession but released with lawyer Jurgen Cooper's help.
280Xio investigates the killings, and Klein prepares to visit the crime scene on Dharavi Street using the All-Black Eye to find Lanevus and avenge Dunn Smith.
281Audrey attends a Beyonder gathering with Fors, while Klein investigates the Dharavi Street crime scene using the All-Black Eye, finding a suspect lurking in the destroyed apartment.
282Klein helps a homeless man in East Borough, highlighting the harsh conditions, and Xio spots the suspect 'Giant' at the Workers Alliance Tavern in the dock area.
283Xio investigates 'Giant' and discovers Lanevus at the Dock Union; Klein confirms Lanevus's location and plans to investigate disguised as a reporter.
284Klein, disguised as a reporter, confirms Lanevus's identity at the Dock Union by sensing the True Creator's aura and reports back to Audrey via the Tarot Club.
285The Nighthawks, led by Crestet Cesimir, attack the Dock Union dormitory; Lanevus reveals he possesses the True Creator's godhood and escapes after a fierce battle.
286Lanevus flees into the sewers, and Klein uses divination to track his escape route, deciding to confront him.
287Klein, wearing a clown mask, confronts and kills Lanevus in the sewers after a intense battle, avenging Dunn Smith and fully digesting his Clown potion.
288Klein escapes the sewers, and the Nighthawks find Lanevus's body covered in tarot cards; Klein reflects on his revenge and advancement.
289Audrey learns about Lanevus's death from Viscount Glaint, and the Nighthawks investigate the mysterious killer while discussing the implications.
290In the Tarot Club, Klein discusses the True Creator's influence and the poor conditions in East Borough, prompting Audrey to vow for change.
291Alger and Klein trade secrets about peak-level Sequences, with Alger agreeing to obtain Beyonder ingredients for Klein in exchange for information on Sequence 0.
292The Tarot Club members exchange news about pirates, including the new Rear Admiral Ailment, and discuss the serial killer case in Backlund.
293Derrick shares information about a statue of the True Creator found in the City of Silver, revealing connections to the Forsaken Land of the Gods.
294Klein invests in Leppard's bicycle project, and Audrey receives a Rainbow Salamander for her dog Susie to advance to Telepathist.
295Klein obtains the Beyonder ingredients from Alger and Derrick, concocts the Magician potion, and advances to Sequence 7, gaining new abilities.
296Klein explores his new abilities as a Magician, including Damage Transfer, Flaming Jump, and other spells, adapting to his enhanced powers.
297During a Blood Moon, Fors suffers from ravings and prays to The Fool, who saves her and invites her to join the Tarot Club as The Magician.
298Fors returns to reality and plans to join the Tarot Club, while Xio remains unaware of the full extent of the events.
299Klein is hired by Rogo Colloman to protect his son Adol, discovering that Adol is haunted by a wraith from Numinous Episcopate rituals.
300Klein exorcises the wraith haunting Adol and learns about a Numinous Episcopate resurrection ritual near Grimm Cemetery, deciding to observe it secretly.
